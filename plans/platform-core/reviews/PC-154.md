### Deviations

1. `copyDiagnostics(trigger = 'button')` takes the trigger, so the 5xx dialog's copy is counted
   as `dialog` in `tm.support.diagnostics-copied`.
2. `registerDumpSection` takes an optional `DestroyRef` rather than injecting one, so a root
   service can call it outside an injection context; without one the section lasts as long as
   the app. `telemetry` and `sections` are reserved, as the dump's own keys.
3. The worker's version shares the storage estimate's 200 ms box (§10.1 names the estimate as
   the only await): reading it needs `/updates`, which may not have loaded yet.
4. `truncated` names a step only when the step dropped something.
5. `TmSupportDialog` is interim: the Diagnostics panel over the snapshot, with Close. The six
   states of §12.2 are PC-210's.
6. The host chunk is preloaded at idle; a press before it lands opens the dialog when it lands,
   not in the press's frame. After the preload (the normal case) the open is synchronous.
7. `TmWorkerUpdates` gains `version`, so the panel and the dump read the worker's version
   through the existing seam.
8. A new `@tellma/locale-ar/support-strings` entry point; `./platform-strings` would have gone
   over its ceiling.
9. The downloaded file is pretty-printed (two spaces), for a human reader; the 2 MiB cap is
   measured on the compact form that is sent.
10. The panel shows request times in the device's local time, `HH:mm:ss`.
11. The dump names a failed section's error class with its own small rule (name, else type),
    not the telemetry pipeline's `tmErrorClass`: exporting that from the primary moved the
    pipeline's whole error capture into the first frame.
12. Spec 0026 §8.4 has `tm-diagnostics` `@defer`red until the tab is selected; the tab imports it
    with `import()` on its first selection instead (spec 0027 §11.1's wording). A deferred block
    cannot show the offline line from another chunk on failure, nor retry on a later selection.
13. §8.4's offline line ships in the support host's chunk, not the primary (D-320); the startup
    preloads that chunk at idle and the worker caches it with the shell, so it is there offline.
14. locale-ar `./shell-strings` rises 2 → 2.5 for the two Arabic labels: they must render before
    `/support` loads, and `tmArabic()` merges its parts by scope, so they cannot live in
    `./support-strings` (WATCHLIST hand-off (d) asked for a ruling or another part).

### Open risks

- If the host chunk itself fails to load (not just `/support`), a press shows nothing; the
  failure goes to telemetry. The chunk is preloaded at idle, so this needs the network to fail
  in the first idle moments and at the press.
- The first-frame figure above.
- The About dialog's placeholder goes from 22rem to 25rem, PC-118's content height plus the tab
  strip computed from the tokens (34 px, its 1 px border, the panel's 12 px). It was not measured
  in a real browser at the dialog's width; a wrong figure shifts content inside the dialog on its
  first open, never the page.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- The press-frame guarantee of `open`: the snapshot is taken before the modal opens, and the
  modal's panel exists synchronously after a preload (`tm-support.spec.ts`).
- The never-list and the truncation order in `tm-support-dump.ts`, and that `page.url` without
  page data drops the query and fragment.
- That nothing of `/support` or its strings is reachable statically from the primary
  (`staticGraphExcludes`), and the first-frame attribution.
- `canSend`'s conditions against §12.1.
