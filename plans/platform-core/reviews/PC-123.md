# PC-123: Home page scaffold: /dashboards entry point, grid and tile, recents widget, TmHomeHost

## Implementer notes

### What was built

**Server** (`src/core/`):

- `FeatureContribution.WidgetType(key)` / `WidgetType<TSettings>(key)` add a
  `WidgetTypeContributionItem`; `WidgetTypeContributionRealizer` records each one on
  `TellmaCompositionRecord.WidgetTypes` with its owner.
- `IWidgetTypeRegistry` (Abstractions `Dashboards/`: `WidgetTypeDescriptor`, `WidgetSubject`) is built from the
  record by `WidgetTypeRegistry` (first registration of a key wins; only `core.report` has subject
  `Report`).
- Startup check `core.widget-types` (`WidgetTypesCheck`, no infrastructure): a malformed key, a key
  not under the registering feature's name, a settings type that is not a concrete closed class,
  and a key registered twice.
- `WidgetTypeClientAssetContributor` writes the Core chunk's `widgetTypes` section, the keys in
  registry order. `CoreFeature` registers the registry, the contributor and `core.recents`.

**Client primary** (`client/projects/core/tellma-core/`):

- `TmDashboardWidgetModel` and `TmTextSlots` beside `tmWidget` (`src/dashboards/tm-widget.ts`).
- `TM_CORE_WIDGETS` (`src/dashboards/tm-core-widgets.ts`): `core.recents`, its body loaded from
  `/dashboards` through a cast specifier; the core feature declares it.
- `TM_ROUTE_HOSTS.home` loads `TmHomeHost` from `/dashboards` (the placeholder is gone), and
  `TmUrls.preload` of a home URL also preloads the app's `home` hatch.
- The home skeleton (`src/routes/tm-page-skeleton.ts`) now draws the greeting's two lines and the
  home grid's three frames with the global classes of `styles/tm-dashboard.css`, the sheet the
  grid itself uses (`@layer tm.base`, container `tm-dashboard`, 768 px threshold, print = wide).
  The frames' placements and the rows' spacing live in that sheet (`.tm-home-skeleton >
  :nth-child(n)`, `.tm-dashboard-frame__rows`), not in the primary's code; the tile's loading view
  reuses the same rows skeleton. A tools test (`client/tools/tests/home-skeleton.spec.mts`) holds
  the sheet's placements to `tmPackDashboard(TM_HOME_WIDGETS)`.
- The dashboard skeleton's column now uses the global `.tm-dashboard-column`.
- `ɵTmPageSkeleton` is exported so `TmHomeHost` can draw the same skeleton while a hatch loads.

**`@tellma/core/dashboards`** (new lazy entry point): `tmPackDashboard`, `TmDashboardGrid`,
`TmDashboardTile`, `TM_HOME_WIDGETS`, `TmHomeGreeting`, `TmHomePage`, `TmHomeHost` and
`ɵTmRecentsWidget`; its English strings (`tm.home` greeting and grid label, `tm.dashboard`) and
its icons register on the first component.

**Arabic**: a new `@tellma/locale-ar/dashboards-strings` entry point holds `home` (title included,
moved out of `./platform-strings`) and `dashboard`; `tmArabic()` loads it as its seventh table.
The client workspace's and the Acme client's `tsconfig.json` map the new entry point to its sources.

**Tooling**: `tm/lazy-layouts` accepts cast specifiers (`import('x' as string) as Promise<M>`);
`tokens:check` lists the tile placement properties (`--tm-col`, `--tm-span`, `--tm-row`,
`--tm-rows`, `--tm-row-narrow`) as runtime-set; the migrator fixture and
`tmTestMetadata` carry `widgetTypes`.

**Acme e2e**: `specs/home.spec.ts`; `ACME_TEXT.home` is now the greeting (a pattern), since the
home's h1 is no longer "Home".

### Deviations

- `TmDashboardWidgetModel.report` is left out: `TmReportRef` does not exist until the reports
  checkpoints.
- `TmDashboardGrid` ships only `widgets`, `label` and `denied`; `parameters`, `dashboardParameters`,
  `dashboardId`, `refreshMinutes`, `view`, `presenting`, `refreshAll`, the tile menu, the status
  line, `injectTmWidget`, the home page context state and telemetry come with the later home
  checkpoint.
- The dashboards' icons register lazily from `/dashboards`, only for names the app lacks, not in
  `platform-icons.ts` (the primary has 0.15 KB of headroom), as the launcher's do.
- Arabic for the home and dashboards lives in a new `./dashboards-strings` entry point instead of
  `./platform-strings`, which may not be raised again; `home.title` moved with it, since the merge
  under `tm` is shallow.
- `TmHomePage` renders only the home widgets whose types the app declares. With `core.recents`
  alone, the wide layout matches the skeleton, but in the narrow layout the recents tile sits at
  row 1 while the skeleton's recents frame sits at row 10, until the assignment types ship.
- A hatch whose chunk fails to load falls back to the platform's home page.
- A widget whose type no declaration knows is titled by its type key.

### Open risks / what to examine hardest

- `TmDashboardTile`'s load effect and its module-level cache: loading → ready, error → retry, a
  tile destroyed mid-load.
- `tmPackDashboard`'s band grouping against spec 0035 §8.2's examples (unit vectors).
- The greeting's timer (next boundary in the display zone) and its `visibilitychange` re-read.
- The narrow-layout skeleton mismatch noted above, and whether the hatch fallback is the wanted
  behaviour.
- Sizes: the primary measures 54.49 KB against its 54.5 ceiling (gzip-9 54.41 of 55), about 6
  bytes of headroom; `/dashboards` 6.16 KB (ceiling 6.5); Arabic `.` 0.54 of 0.55 and
  `./dashboards-strings` 0.34 (ceiling 0.5). The notes sit beside the ceilings in the
  `package.json` budgets.
- Acme e2e: `keyboard-errors:67` (an in-app navigation from the picker to the home) failed in
  both plain full runs, in both languages, and `automatic-entries:107` in one; both are on the
  ledger's watch list of local full-run flakes, and `keyboard-errors:67` passed alone. Its trace
  shows the page's requests for the home's chunks, and `client/sync`, held while the service
  worker installs its prefetch group; the home now needs the `/dashboards` chunks, where the
  placeholder needed none beyond `/shell`.
- The skeleton's placements are duplicated in CSS; the tools test is the only thing that keeps
  them in step with `TM_HOME_WIDGETS`.

R1-1 (blocking). **`keyboard-errors:67` fails every plain full run because of this checkpoint.
The cause is the Angular worker's localhost-only first initialization, not a flake.**
`distributions/acme/test/Tellma.Distro.Acme.E2E/specs/keyboard-errors.spec.ts:67-79`.

- **Mechanism, from the ngsw source this build ships.** `ngsw-worker.js`'s
  `scheduleInitialization` runs `appVersion.initializeFully()` inline when
  `isLocalhost(registration.scope)`. On any other origin it schedules that for idle.
  `handleFetch` awaits `ensureInitialized` before it answers anything. So on
  `https://localhost:<port>`, every fetch the page makes after the worker claims it waits for the
  whole `shell` prefetch group. That group is 102 files, about 857 KB gzip (`workerShell` in the
  build), fetched one after another, and each fetch goes through the CSP fixture's route.
- **Trace of the failing chromium run.** I have it locally and can attach it.
  - 05.505: the worker registers on `/tenants`.
  - 05.798: Enter is pressed on the Acme link.
  - 05.926–05.992: the page requests `chunk-5PFPQVK6.js`, which is the `/dashboards` chunk
    (`tm-home-host`), together with `client/sync` and the shell panels. None of them completes.
  - 06.02–10.78: the worker's prefetch walks the whole group, ending with `index.html`.
  - 11.02: the 5 s `toBeFocused` gives up, with an empty `main`.
- **Why this checkpoint.** Before it, the home's `h1` came from the placeholder in `./shell`,
  which `/tenants` had already loaded. Now the `h1` needs a chunk fetched inside that window.
  Under six local workers the prefetch takes more than 5 s, so the test fails every full run. Alone,
  the prefetch finishes in time. That is the "passes alone" pattern, but it is deterministic under
  load.
- **Production is unaffected.** A real origin initializes at idle, and prefetch-group fetches go
  to the network while initialization is pending. This is therefore a harness fix, not a product
  preload. A preload of the home chunk would be held behind the same prefetch.
- **Fix.** The repository already names this exact cause and remedy. `launch.spec.ts:33-37` says
  "On a localhost scope the Angular worker prefetches its whole shell group before it answers any
  fetch…" and uses `test.use({ serviceWorkers: 'block' })` for tests that are not about the
  worker. Apply the same in keyboard-errors' describe, since none of its tests is about the
  worker, with that comment. The other option is `await warmUp(page, '/tenants')` in place of
  `page.goto('/tenants')` and the visibility wait: a named condition, meaning the controller is
  set and the shell cache is full, as layout-shift.spec does. Neither raises a timeout, and neither
  loosens the focus assertion, because the home chunk still loads lazily after the Enter.
- **`automatic-entries:107` (failed in chromium-ar here).** The same mechanism, but the trace does
  not tie it to the home chunk.
  - The `/dashboards` chunk loaded at 02.609, before the worker registered at 03.01.
  - The tenant-not-found redirect then waited on a held request for `chunk-7FVRRZQQ.js`, a shared
    core-ui chunk. The worker's prefetch released it at 06.96, after the 5 s `toHaveURL` window.
  - It is the ledger's existing watch row. Its describe is not about the worker either, so the same
    `serviceWorkers: 'block'` line fixes it at the cause.
  - I recommend landing both in this checkpoint's fix round, so the ledger row (with
    `cold-deep-link:84`, which passed here) can be re-judged on the root cause rather than as
    flakes.

R1-2 (minor). **The home e2e times the recents dwell with a wall-clock sleep from the wrong
start.** `distributions/acme/test/Tellma.Distro.Acme.E2E/specs/home.spec.ts:51-54`.

- **What the test does.** `toHaveURL(/\/1\/centers$/)` passes when the router commits the URL.
  `TmRecents` starts its 2 s dwell only when the Centers page context registers, after the search
  chunk loads and renders (`recents/src/tm-recents.ts:190-217`). The 2.5 s sleep therefore leaves
  0.5 s for that load.
- **Why it can fail.** This test also starts on a fresh worker: `tmSignInToIdentityAs`, then
  `goto('/')`. So the search chunk can sit behind R1-1's prefetch hold for seconds. The visit is
  then never recorded, and `link` never appears.
- **It breaks the suite's own rule.** The README says "wait on observable state … never on the
  clock".
- **Fix.**
  - Wait for the Centers page's `h1` before timing the dwell.
  - Either drive the dwell with `page.clock`, or add `test.use({ serviceWorkers: 'block' })` with
    R1-1's comment, since this test is not about the worker.
  - Then wait for the link with an expectation rather than a fixed sleep.

R1-3 (minor). **A `home` hatch never receives the arrival focus on an in-app navigation, while its
chunk is not yet in `TmHomeHost`'s cache.** `client/projects/core/tellma-core/dashboards/src/tm-home-host.ts:53-73`.

- **Failure.** The route host releases its focus hold in the `afterNextRender` after
  `TmHomeHost` first renders (`src/routes/tm-route-host.ts:412`, `tm-page-focus.ts:85-113`). At
  that point `TmHomeHost` shows the skeleton, which has no `[tmPageTitle]`, so `TmPageFocus`
  focuses `main`. The hatch's `h1` arrives a microtask later and never gets focus.
- **When it happens.** On every first in-app entry to home in a session, for every distribution
  with a hatch. Even a hatch module that `TmUrls.preload` has already loaded misses, because
  `hatch()` resolves only after the render and the release. This breaks spec 0024 §4.4's arrival
  focus and WCAG 2.4.3.
- **Fix.** Either option keeps the primary's bytes unchanged:
  - `TmHomeHost` takes a focus hold while the hatch loads and releases it in the `afterNextRender`
    after `page.set`. `TmPageFocus.ɵhold` would need a `ɵ` export.
  - The dashboards entry point resolves the hatch before handing `TmHomeHost` to the route, for
    example with a module-level promise the loader awaits.
- **Test.** Extend tm-home.spec's hatch case with an in-app navigation from another route,
  asserting the hatch's `h1` is focused.

R1-4 (minor). **A loading tile is not marked busy.** `client/projects/core/tellma-core/dashboards/src/tm-dashboard-tile.ts:66`
and `:87-90`.

- **The rule.** Spec 0035 §18's WCAG row says "`aria-busy` while loading". `tm-skeleton` is
  `aria-hidden`, and its a11y notes say "the page region it sits in carries `aria-busy="true"`".
- **Failure.** While the body's chunk loads, the tile's `section` exposes its heading over
  content that assistive technology cannot see, with no busy state, so a screen reader cannot tell
  a loading list from an empty one.
- **Fix.** Add `[attr.aria-busy]="view() === 'loading' ? 'true' : null"` to the section, with an
  assertion in the grid spec's skeleton-rows case.

R1-5 (minor). **The greeting changes an hour late after a spring-forward night.**
`client/projects/core/tellma-core/dashboards/src/tm-home-greeting.ts:187-191`, `:255-259`.

- **Cause.** `untilNextBoundary` subtracts wall-clock times of day.
- **Failure.** Take a zone that springs forward between midnight and 05:00, such as Europe at
  02:00→03:00 or America at 02:00. A timer set at 00:30 for 05:00 waits 4.5 real hours. It fires at
  06:00 wall time, so "Good evening" shows from 05:00 to 06:00. A fall-back night fires early and
  re-arms, which is harmless.
- **Fix.** Cap the delay at one hour, so a DST jump costs at most one re-arm. It stays one timer,
  as §15.2 asks. Add a test with a fake `TM_NOW` and a DST zone.

R1-6 (minor). **`TmDashboardTile`'s public API, now approved in the golden, types its required
input with an unexported type.** `client/projects/core/tellma-core/dashboards/src/tm-dashboard-tile.ts:134`,
`:152`; `client/api/core.dashboards.api.md:27-41`.

- **The problem.** `tile: InputSignal<TmTileModel>` and `view: Signal<TmTileView>` are public,
  but neither type is exported. `ae-forgotten-export` is silenced, so api:check did not flag it.
  A hatch or distribution cannot name the model `TmDashboardTile` requires, though spec 0035 §1.1
  lists the tile as a main-module export.
- **Why it matters now.** The golden freezes an input shape that PC-167 will change when it adds
  the status line and menu.
- **Fix.** Either export `TmTileModel` and `TmTileView`, with TSDoc, as part of the contract, or
  make the tile an internal `ɵTmDashboardTile` until PC-167 defines its public inputs.

R1-7 (minor). **Several rules the change implements have no test that fails when they break.**

- **The active content slot.** The grid's title rule reads the active slot, else slot 1
  (`tm-dashboard-grid.ts:288-296`). The greeting's name does the same
  (`tm-home-greeting.ts:228-236`). But every title in `tm-dashboard-grid.spec.ts:217-255` is in
  slot 1, and tm-home.spec has no slot-2 user. Code that always read slot 1 passes both. Add a
  case with the content slot set to 2, for a tile title and for the greeting's name.
- **The tile's load lifecycle.** The implementer's own open risk is "a tile destroyed mid-load".
  Two behaviours are untested: a load that settles after the tile is gone, which must render
  nothing and throw nothing (`tm-dashboard-tile.ts:217-236`), and the module cache, where a second
  tile of a loaded type renders its body in its first frame. Add one spec for each.
- **`TmDashboardGrid.tiles`.** Only `status: 'ready'` is asserted. The `loading`, `error`,
  `denied` and `unavailable` mappings feed PC-167's page context. Assert them in the existing
  state cases.
