# PC-028: Persist frame, error taxonomy, retry, commit probe, instruments and fault injection

## Implementer notes

### What was built, and where

- **The persist frame** (0012 §5.5), in `Tellma.Core/Data/Batches`:
  - `DataBatch.Assemble()` now emits, for a writing `Persist` batch that carries the connect
    prologue, the transaction head (statements in `BatchSection.TransactionHead`, such as spec
    0014's application lock, refused when the text opens no transaction), then `PersistChecks`
    (steps 4 and 5), then the
    body, the epilogue and `COMMIT`. `PersistChecks.Compose` builds the version-tag guard from
    `GuardPremises` (settings and permissions, or settings alone for `System`), every `DependsOn`
    dependency, and `Bumped` from the written tables resolved through `IVersionTagRegistry` plus
    the explicit bumps (two disagreeing expectations of one name become `Guid.Empty`, so the guard
    fails). The guard has a `REPEATABLEREAD` arm for names the batch only reads and an `UPDLOCK`
    arm for names it bumps; the user arm reads `core.UserStamps` (under `UPDLOCK` when the batch
    writes `UserStamps` or a table with a user rule) against `@tm_ExpectedUserPermissionsTag`.
    The caller re-check (`THROW 50401`) follows on a user batch only.
  - `GuardPremises` (`ForUser`/`ForSystem`), handed over by the prologue's contributor through
    `MarkConnectPrologue(GuardPremises)`; `DataBatch.TagParameter()` binds `@tm_tag` for spec
    0013's bump epilogue; `DataBatch.DeclareInsertedRoots(table, ids)` (int and long) names the
    commit probe's rows; `StatementDraft.EntityType` names the entity a tree statement maintains.
  - `IVersionTagRegistry`, `VersionTagEffects`, `UserVersionTagRule` in
    `Tellma.Core.Abstractions/Caching` (spec 0013's contract), resolved optionally by the
    executor.
- **The error taxonomy** (0012 §6.3–§6.4): `DataErrors.Classify` (reported transient,
  ambiguous, final) and `DataErrors.Translate` (platform band, distribution band, provider
  errors, out-of-band numbers → `InvalidOperationException`); the exceptions in
  `Tellma.Core/Data/DataAccessException.cs`, `DataStatementExceptions.cs` and
  `DataAccessFailureExceptions.cs`, every statement exception carrying `StatementOrdinal` from the
  line map. 50409 and 50404 read their preceding result set through `ResultSetCursor.NextIfAsync`
  and `ReadConflictsAsync` / `ReadMissingIdsAsync` (the conflict rows and the missing ids ride on
  the exception). `TellmaSqlErrors` gained the stable message codes.
- **Retry and the commit probe** (0012 §6.5): `RetryRules.Decide` / `Delay` (pure),
  `CommitProbe` (one autocommit `READCOMMITTEDLOCK` count over the first declared root table's
  ids, on its own connection, never cancelled), and `DataExecutor.RunAsync`, the attempt loop:
  per-attempt span, phase tracking (connecting, sent, committing), a lost connection detected on
  the client, rollback unless committing, the probe, the `PK_<Table>` collision after a
  `NotCommitted` verdict, jittered delays on the `TimeProvider`, exhaustion. Results a reader
  produces are staged on the cursor and applied only on the attempt that settles the round trip,
  so a re-run never completes a `BatchResult<T>` twice.
- **Instruments** (0012 §12.1–§12.2): `DataMetrics` on the `Tellma.Core` meter through
  `IMeterFactory` (`roundtrips`, `roundtrip.duration` by `ok`/`retried`/`failed`,
  `batch.statements`, `retries` by `class`/`error`, `commit.probes` by `outcome`), and the scoped
  `DataAccessRecorder`, which records `tellma.data.roundtrips` and stamps it on the current
  `Activity` when the unit of work's scope ends. `DataComposition` registers both.
- **Tests**: unit tests in `test/core/Tellma.Core.Tests/Data/Batches` (`RetryRulesTests`,
  `CommitProbeTests`, `DataErrorsTests`, `PersistFrameTests`, `ResultSetCursorTests`,
  `DataMetricsTests`, plus the updated assembly tests). Integration tests in
  `test/core/Tellma.Core.IntegrationTests/Data`: `DataBatchTests` gained the stale-tag, caller,
  `System`, out-of-band, arithmetic, duplicate-key, CHECK, conflict-guard, exhaustion and
  instrument cases; `DataBatchFaultTests` injects the faults of 0012 §6.5 deterministically (a
  ready statement reports the session id before a gate the test holds; `KILL` from a master
  connection; a client-side close after the server committed; a recording connection that hands
  the test the batch's connection; a `DEADLOCK_PRIORITY HIGH` rival; a `MeterListener` that
  commits the probed row after a `NotCommitted` verdict).

### Deviations (also in the commit message)

1. The version-tag registry contract (`IVersionTagRegistry`, `VersionTagEffects`,
   `UserVersionTagRule`) is declared here, in `Tellma.Core.Abstractions.Caching`, ahead of spec
   0013's checkpoint, because the guard's `Bumped` flags need it. The executor resolves it
   optionally; with none registered only explicit bumps are flagged.
2. Steps 4 and 5 are composed by the executor from `GuardPremises`, which the connect prologue's
   contributor passes to `MarkConnectPrologue(GuardPremises)`; the prologue still declares
   `@tm_UserId` and binds `@tm_ExpectedUserPermissionsTag` for a user.
3. The re-checks run on a writing persist in every transaction mode, not only in the frame of
   `Auto`; in `None` their lock hints last one statement, in `Explicit` they hold to the caller's
   commit. A persist that declares no write opens no transaction and carries no re-checks.
4. `TransactionHead` statements are refused when the text opens no transaction.
5. A non-SQL failure after the send of a writing batch (a reader's defect, for instance) is
   classified as ambiguous: `COMMIT` may have run, so the probe answers, or `Unknown` when there
   is nothing to probe. On a batch that writes nothing it surfaces as is.
6. A statement that declares no write counts as safe to run twice after a lost connection, as
   an `Idempotent` one does.
7. `Explicit`: a failure before the send (opening the connection) is re-run like a reported
   transient failure, since nothing reached the server; after the send a transient or lost
   connection surfaces at once as `DataAccessRetryExhaustedException` with `Attempts = 1`, and a
   connection lost while committing as `DataAccessAmbiguousException(Unknown)`.
8. 547 on a `CHECK` constraint is a plain `DataAccessException`; only a foreign key becomes
   `ForeignKeyViolationException`.
9. A final error before the send (a login failure, say) surfaces as the provider's exception,
   untranslated: no statement ran and there is no ordinal to attribute.
10. The `Committed` verdict after a `NotCommitted` probe is reached by 2627 on the primary key of
    the probed root table only, the one table whose keys the probe knows.
11. 50404 is `BatchAssertionFailedException(50404, "Entity.NotFound")` carrying the missing ids
    from the preceding result set (`MissingIds`), which the pipeline maps to `NotFoundException`.
12. `DataAccessRecorder` is created with a scope's first batch, so a scope that runs no batch
    records no `tellma.data.roundtrips` measurement.
13. A probe that itself fails yields `Unknown` (logged), which the spec leaves open.

### Open risks

- The fault tests depend on SQL Server's behaviour after `KILL`: the client sees a severe error
  or a closed connection, which the executor classifies as ambiguous. If a driver version starts
  surfacing a `KILL` as a class-11 error on an open connection, those tests would fail rather than
  pass silently.
- The deadlock test relies on the rival's `DEADLOCK_PRIORITY HIGH` to pick the batch as the
  victim; the lock monitor's detection interval (up to 5 s) bounds its duration.
- `Classify` treats every SQL error class ≥ 20 and a closed connection as a lost connection; a
  server that reports a severe error but keeps the session (rare) would be probed needlessly.
- The persist re-checks in `None` mode are best-effort (no lock outlives the statement); a
  composer that needs the guarantee must use `Auto`.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- `DataExecutor.RunAsync` / `AttemptAsync`: the order of the cancellation check, classification,
  the PK-collision check and `RetryRules.Decide`; that persist cancellation is ignored once any
  attempt was sent; that rollback is skipped while committing; that `Settle` applies staged
  results only on the settling attempt.
- `RetryRules.Decide` against the table of 0012 §6.5, especially the `Explicit` and `None` arms.
- `PersistChecks.Compose`: the arms' lock hints, the conflict-to-`Guid.Empty` rule, the user arm's
  `UPDLOCK` rule, and the `System` differences.
- `DataErrors.Translate`: the band handling, the duplicate-key and foreign-key name extraction in
  non-English messages, and `StatementOrdinal` resolution (null inside a procedure).
- `DataBatchFaultTests`: whether each test injects its fault at the moment it claims (gate,
  ready statement, `IsBlockedByAsync`) and whether any can pass for the wrong reason. The class
  passed three consecutive runs locally (8 tests, 0.1–4 s each).
- `tellma.data.commit.probes` records every verdict, including the two reached without a probe
  round trip (`unknown` when nothing was declared to probe, `committed` from the duplicate key
  after a `not-committed` probe), as 0012 §6.5 lists them under the one instrument.
