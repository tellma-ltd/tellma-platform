# PC-109: Primary attention seams: TmBanners, TmInputIdle, platform banners, attention documents

## Implementer notes

### What was built, and where

All client-side, under `client/projects/core/` unless noted. No C# changes.

- **Primary `@tellma/core`, `tellma-core/src/attention/`** (spec 0028 §9.1-§9.3, §9.5, §5.1, §8.1):
  - `tm-banners.ts`: `TmBannerLevel`, `TmBannerOptions`, `TmBannerRef`, `TmBannerView` and the
    root service `TmBanners` (`show`, `current`, `visible`). Entries sort by level, then by the
    order `show` first ran. The head is the first entry that is not dismissed. The row shows the
    head, or the entry the user paged to (`ɵpage`, wrapping). That choice sticks until the entry
    closes or is dismissed, or until a newer banner of a higher level arrives. `show` with a live
    id updates the entry in place and returns the same ref. `ref.update` cannot change `id` or
    `level`, and `ref.close` removes the entry; both are no-ops after a close.
    - Dismissal (`ɵdismiss`) holds for the tab's life. Internal `ɵshow(options, scope)` scopes a
      dismissal: the update banner passes its version, so a newer version shows again.
    - One `#settle` routine recomputes two views after every change. `ɵhead` is ungated and is
      what the host announces from. `current`/`visible` change the row's height (open or close)
      only when one of these holds: the change is the user's own dismiss, `TmInputIdle.idle()` is
      true, or the page is hidden. Otherwise the routine waits once on `whenIdle()`. A swap in an
      open row applies at once. An emptied row stays open with `current() === null` until the
      gate. A `visibilitychange` to hidden applies a waiting change.
    - Development throws for a non-`tm.` id at levels 1-3 and for `dismissible: true` at
      levels 1-2. Production shows both, forcing `dismissible: false` at levels 1-2.
    - Telemetry: the timing `tm.attention.banner { id, level }`, from the first `show` to the row
      first showing the entry, once per entry.

    - Five passive capture listeners on the window: keydown, pointerdown, pointermove, wheel and
      touchstart.

### Deviations

1. The update banner's source is not shipped (planner note: it moves to PC-110), although spec
   0028 §9.5 says `provideTellma` starts both sources. The per-version dismissal it needs is
   shipped as the internal `TmBanners.ɵshow(options, scope)`. The §9.1 contract has no member
   for it, and a scope parameter keeps the public `show` exactly as specified.
2. `TmBanners` has internal `ɵhead`, `ɵdismiss` and `ɵpage`, which §9.1 does not list. The host
   needs them to announce at queue time (§9.4) and to wire the bar's outputs back.
3. The host announces when the row's banner changes to another id. That includes the user's own
   paging, not only "when an entry becomes the head" (§9.4). Otherwise a screen-reader user who
   presses Next hears nothing. A `ref.update` or a message change of the same banner is not
   re-announced.
4. `TmInputIdle` starts busy and turns idle 2 s after it is created. §9.3 defines idle as 2 s
   without input, and the time before the service existed cannot be observed.
5. A dismissible level-1/2 banner and a distribution banner at levels 1-3 both throw in
   development. §9.1 states only the latter; the former enforces "never for levels 1 and 2". In
   production the first is forced non-dismissible and the second shows as given.
6. `TmApiHost.listen` gained an optional options argument, an additive change to an internal
   seam.
7. Budgets, under D-031:
   - `"."` raised from 45 to 46.5: measured 44.81 before and 46.23 after (+1.42 KB, within
     0028's 2.5 KB share and spec 0024's 50 KB total).
   - `@tellma/locale-ar/strings` raised from 6.5 to 7 (measured 6.51) for the one Arabic string.
   - PC-111 also raises `"."` (to 47 by its notes). Whichever of PC-111 and PC-109 integrates
     second re-measures (D-214).

### Open risks

- **`assignments/summary` does not exist on the server until PC-207.** `provideTellma`
  registers the document as spec 0028 §5.1 says. So until then, every leader refresh of it (on
  election, on reconnect, and every 60 s in degraded mode) gets `404 route-not-found`. The call
  is judged by the outdated-client verdict (`contract-mismatch` when the builds match) and
  reported as a `tm-leader` telemetry error. No UI shows it, since these are background reads.
  An e2e that asserts on telemetry errors or failed requests would see it. The other option was
  to register only the inbox until PC-207; that ruling is the orchestrator's.
- The offline banner is not visible anywhere until the shells render the host. No e2e covers
  §12's "5 s offline while typing" case yet (0028-h).
- A dismiss that empties the row removes the focused button, and focus falls to `<body>`. The
  spec is silent; noted for PC-155.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- `#settle` in `tm-banners.ts`: the gate conditions, the single pending `whenIdle` wait and its
  re-check, the empty-open-row state, and the outrank rule in `ɵshow`. The rule compares against
  the view computed after the new entry is added. With no page chosen that is the natural head,
  and with a page chosen it is the paged entry.
- The scoped dismissal: `#showable` hides an id only when the stored scope equals the entry's
  current scope.
- `TmInputIdle`'s timer arithmetic, and that a pointermove stream causes no timer churn.
- The host's politeness switch and its 100 ms delayed write, in place of two regions.
- The +1.42 KB on `"."`: whether any of it could leave the first frame. My view is no. The
  offline source and `TmBanners` run from startup in every shell, `TmInputIdle` is shared with
  the time-zone prompt, and the host is already out.

### Findings

**R1-1 (major): `assignments/summary` is registered while the route does not exist, and PC-207 is Tier 3.**
`tm-attention-documents.ts:104` and `provide-tellma.ts:109` register the `assignments` leader
document in every app. The server maps no such route: only `WebApiProjection.cs`'s
`PollingRoutes` names it. So every leader refresh 404s with `route-not-found`. That covers
election, every reconnect, any `assignments.changed`, and every 60 s degraded poll. The SPA reads
`route-not-found` as its sign of an outdated client (`RouteMisses.cs:19`).
- *Equal builds* (the normal deployment): `#verdict` returns `contract-mismatch`, and
  `tm-http-api.ts:1129-1131` reports it through `TmTelemetry.error`. Once PC-112's pipeline lands,
  this is a steady stream of false "contract bug" errors from production. They dilute the one
  signal that is meant to catch real contract breaks.
- *Unequal or unordered builds* (a slot swap, a rollback): the miss runs the outdated-client
  verdict. That means `tm.api.client-outdated` events, an update check or a worker probe of up to
  3 s, and a `server-outdated` retry. A missing feature then looks like version skew.
- The notes and the WATCHLIST PC-207 line say the error is reported "as a `tm-leader` telemetry
  error". That is wrong: `tm-leadership.ts:478` deliberately skips `TmApiError`. The reporter is
  the HTTP layer.
- PC-207 is Tier 3 (CHECKPOINTS.md), so it can fall below the spill line, and the MVP would then
  ship this behaviour for good.

*Recommendation (the orchestrator's point (c)):* register only the inbox until PC-207.
- Drop the `assignments` entry from `TM_ATTENTION_DOCUMENTS`, and record it as a deviation from
  §5.1, justified like the update banner.
- Keep `TmAssignmentsSummary`, `TmLaunchDocs.assignments` and `TmLaunchCache.docs.assignments`.
  They are types, or a signal that reads `undefined`, and cost nothing.
- Change the WATCHLIST PC-207 line to "add `{ name: 'assignments', … }` to
  `TM_ATTENTION_DOCUMENTS` once the route is mapped".
- Update `provide-tellma.spec.ts:87` (formats `{ inbox: 1 }`), `tm-attention-documents.spec.ts`,
  the README paragraph and ARCHITECTURE.md's row.
- This saves 0.02 KB on ".", measured.

**R1-2 (minor): the outrank rule in `ɵshow` drops the user's page when nothing new takes the row, and shows the head instead of the new banner.**
`tm-banners.ts:200-204` runs for every `show`. That includes an in-place update of a live id and a
`show` of a dismissed id. It also clears `#paged`, so the row falls back to the head and does not
show the banner that outranked the page.
- *Scenario 1:* the user pages to `acme.tip` (level 5). A distribution then refreshes the message
  of its live `acme.warn` (level 4) through `show()`, which 0028 §9.1 allows ("show() with a live
  id updates it"). Or it re-shows a dismissed `acme.warn`. In both cases the row jumps back to
  the head, though nothing new appeared, and the user's paging, which §9.2 says "sticks", is lost.
- *Scenario 2:* `tm.offline` (level 1) is the head and the user pages to `acme.tip` (level 5). A
  new `acme.warn` (level 4) arrives. §9.2 says a banner of a higher level "takes the row at once".
  Here the row shows `tm.offline`, and `acme.warn` is reachable only through the pager.
- *Fix:*
  - Apply the rule only when the entry is newly created or newly showable.
  - When it outranks the shown view, set `#paged` to that entry. Use `null` when it is the
    natural head.
  - Add tests for both scenarios. The current test at `tm-banners.spec.ts:114-117` passes only
    because `tm.update` happens to be the head.

**R1-3 (minor): `TmInputIdle` runs on the wall clock, so moving the system clock back freezes the gate.**
`tm-input-idle.ts:97` computes `left = #last + 2000 - #now()` with `TM_NOW` (`Date.now`).
- *Scenario:* the clock moves back by X while the app is open. That can be a manual change, a
  large NTP correction, or a VM or laptop resync after sleep. `left` becomes about 2 s + X, and
  `#check` re-arms for that long.
- For X = 1 h, `idle()` stays false for an hour. The offline banner is announced but never drawn,
  the row never closes, and 0024 §7.8's time-zone prompt never opens.
- *Fix:* clamp the re-arm with `this.#arm(Math.min(left, TM_INPUT_IDLE_MS))`, or measure with
  `performance.now()`. Add a fake-clock test that calls `vi.setSystemTime` backwards after an input.

**R1-4 (minor, the orchestrator's point (e)): duplicate leader-document names are unguarded, and the token's own example is now a duplicate.**
The omission in `provideTellmaStateTesting()` is fine for tests. The leader rig's
`TM_INBOX_DOC` has a different `pollWhile` that its specs rely on. But the 9 failures the
implementer saw are the production effect itself, not a test-only clash.
- `TmLeadership` (`leader/src/tm-leadership.ts:177`) builds one read slot per registration with
  no de-duplication. Two registrations of a name therefore mean two requests on every trigger,
  both `put` into the same stored document. If their routes differ, two shapes alternate.
- `TM_STORE_EXTRA_FORMATS` (`tm-doc-formats.ts:48`) keeps whichever `formatVersion` comes last.
- `TM_LEADER_DOCUMENTS` is public (`public-api.ts:260`). Its TSDoc example
  (`tm-leader-documents.ts:33-36`) registers `{ name: 'inbox', route: 'inbox/summary', … }`,
  which since this commit duplicates the platform's own registration. A distribution that copies
  the example silently doubles every inbox read.
- *Fix:*
  - Give the example a distribution name, such as `acme.approvals` on `acme/approvals/summary`.
  - Throw in development, and keep the first in production, when two registrations share a name.
    The `TM_STORE_EXTRA_FORMATS` factory, or `TmLeadership`'s constructor, is the one place for it.
  - Add a test for the duplicate.

**R1-5 (minor): the live region keeps a closed banner's text.**
`tm-banner-host.ts:106-118` sets `announcement` when a banner becomes the head, and never clears
it. When the head turns `null`, it only resets `announced`.
- *Scenario:* after "Connection lost. Reconnecting…" closes, a screen-reader user reading the
  header in browse mode still finds that text in the hidden region, minutes after the connection
  returned.
- *Fix:* empty `announcement` (and clear the timer) when the head becomes `null`. Add an assertion
  to `tm-banner-host.spec.ts`.

**R1-6 (minor): three rules have no pinning test.**
- *`provideTellma` starts the offline source (§9.5, "in every shell").* Deleting
  `provide-tellma.ts:110` fails nothing. `tm-platform-banners.spec.ts` installs
  `tmPlatformBanners()` itself, and `provide-tellma.spec.ts:87` checks only the documents.
  *Fix:* with `provideTellma(config)` and an offline fake API, advance 5 s and expect
  `TmBanners.ɵhead()` to be `tm.offline`.
- *A change undone before the gate never moves the row.* The notes ask reviewers to examine the
  single pending wait and its re-check, but no test covers them. *Fix:* while typing, `show`
  then `close` should leave `visible()` false through the next idle moment. On an open row,
  `close` then `show` of another banner should never flip `visible()` to false.
- *`TmLaunchCache.docs.inbox` and `.assignments`* (0025 §3.1) are read by nothing in the tests.
  *Fix:* one assertion in the launch-cache spec after `tmWarmTenant` with both summaries.

### Answers to the orchestrator's points

- **(a) The "." raise.** I measured 46.23 against 44.81 at the base (4bd4bbd2), so +1.42 KB, and
  46.5 is that rounded up to 0.5 KB. That is honest under D-031, within 0028's 2.5 KB share, and it
  adds no dependency. To see what each piece costs, I removed it from the built FESM and
  re-measured with the gate's own measurer:
  - the offline source with its `wifi-off` icon, 0.28 KB (the icon alone is 0.12);
  - `TmInputIdle`'s listeners and timer, 0.16;
  - the pager and dismiss bodies, 0.06;
  - the `assignments` registration, 0.02.
  The rest is the `TmBanners` core, the gate and the offline string.
  - **What must stay eager.** Everything here is first-frame code, and nothing can honestly move
    to a lazy entry point. The offline source and its string must be loaded before the network
    goes away, and a lazy chunk cannot load while offline. `TmInputIdle` must listen from startup.
    0028 §1 puts `TmBanners` in the primary so the primary's own code can raise banners
    synchronously.
  - **What could move, and why it should not.** Moving the icon or the pager into `./attention`
    lowers "." on paper only, because `./attention` is modulepreloaded on every first frame. D-205
    rejects that kind of move. The one move that is mechanically possible is a TmLeader-style
    facade, with `TmInputIdle`'s listeners in a chunk loaded after first render. It would save at
    most 0.16 KB, and it adds a load-failure path in which the gate never opens. I do not
    recommend it.
  - **What to take.** The realistic saving is R1-1's 0.02 KB.
  - **Projection (D-247).** platform-core is at 44.87 (PC-117 included). Adding PC-109 (+1.42)
    gives about 46.29, still under 46.5. Adding PC-111 (+2.27) and PC-115 (+1.82) gives about 50.38

    0028's page declarations still fall inside 0028's remaining ~1.1 KB share.
