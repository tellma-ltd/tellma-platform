# PC-F112: Pager page-size select on demand (from PC-128)

## Implementer notes

### What was built

Fix item from PC-129's blocked integration (D-389). It lands PC-128's reviewed pager change on its
own: `tm-pager`'s page-size `tm-select` loads on demand behind a stand-in that replays input. The
sources are pc/PC-128's 2a52ed80 (route saving) and the pager parts of its fix1 e7f53338 (the
handover from R1-6), plus fix2 94b802aa's correction to the e2e helper `holdSelectChunks`. Nothing
of the search field, type-to-search, `./search-view` or the search host's new behaviour comes over.

Platform-core's copies of every file the change touches were identical to PC-128's base f2630982,
apart from PC-F91's per-icon Lucide imports in `tm-pager.ts`. So each file is PC-128's reviewed
version (at e7f53338, and 94b802aa for `support/acme.ts`), with one adaptation: `tm-pager.ts`
imports `ChevronDown`, `ChevronLeft` and `ChevronRight` from `lucide/dist/esm/icons/*.mjs` under
the `@tellma/core-tools/lucide-icons` reference, as platform-core does everywhere. The pager's
behaviour is PC-128's reviewed version byte for byte otherwise.

- `client/projects/core/tellma-core-ui/pager/tm-pager.ts`: `tm-select` inside
  `@defer (when sizeSelectWanted())`. The pager asks for idle itself (`afterNextRender`, then
  `requestIdleCallback`, else `setTimeout`) instead of the block's `on idle`, whose scheduler
  would join the app's first frame. The stand-in `.tm-pager__proxy` is a collapsed
  `role="combobox"` named "Page size" in the same tab stop (`tabindex` -1 and `aria-disabled`
  while disabled) and shows the current size's label (or the select's placeholder). A pointer
  over it, focus, a press or a key loads the select. The handover runs in an
  `afterRenderEffect`: the select renders inert for one pass, then the stand-in retires. A
  focused stand-in that nothing was asked of stays until it blurs. One that was asked for
  something hands focus straight to the trigger before it leaves. A press keeps it until the
  press ends in a click. On the next pass typed text goes to `seed(text)` and a click or an open
  key to `open()`.
- `tm-pager.css`: `--tm-pager-block-size` on the size ladder and the stand-in's chrome (height,
  padding, border, radius, background, text, caret, hover, focus glow, disabled).
- `tm-pager.spec.ts`: PC-128's 9 tests with the select held by `DeferBlockBehavior.Manual`. They
  cover box, chrome and size at sm, md and lg, the press guard, an open key, typeahead, the
  focused stand-in kept, the trigger focused straight from the stand-in with no stop on the
  body, and the disabled stand-in.
- `client/projects/core/tellma-core-ui-testing/pager-harness.ts`: `getSizeSelect()` points at the
  stand-in and waits, bounded, for the select to land.
- `client/api/core-ui.pager.api.md`: the new protected members.
- `@tellma/core-ui` `package.json`: `./pager` from 2.8 to 4.1 (PC-128's raise, D-371), with
  PC-128's budget note, and `staticGraphExcludes["./pager"]` gains `./select`.
  `@tellma/core` `package.json`: `staticGraphExcludes["./search"]` gains
  `@tellma/core-ui/select`.
- `@tellma/core-ui` README's pager line; ARCHITECTURE.md's component-library paragraph gains one
  sentence on the pager's deferred select and its static-graph guard. PC-128 did not add it.
- Acme e2e: `support/acme.ts` `holdSelectChunks` (with fix2's drop of a request whose response
  was disposed); `layout-shift.spec.ts` "the pager's size select replaces its stand-in with zero
  shift" with the `recordShifts`/`settledShifts` helpers it shares with the first test;
  `search-page.spec.ts` "a click on the page size before its select loads opens it when it
  loads".

### Left out, and why

- e7f53338's `tm-select-base.ts` change (R1-1: the closed trigger calls `preventDefault()` on the
  key it takes for its typeahead). It exists for type-to-search, which platform-core does not
  have. The pager's behaviour does not depend on it: the stand-in marks its own keys, and the
  loaded select's typeahead works either way. It comes with PC-128.
- Everything in 2a52ed80 and e7f53338 outside the pager's files: the search field, type-to-search,
  `./search-view`, the host, the grid's `menuRowId`, `TmErrorState.live` and PC-128's own review
  file.

### Deviations

1. The pager's size select is not in its first render. A stand-in of its geometry and name holds
   its place until it loads at idle or on first use (D-364, as PC-128).
2. Ceiling: `@tellma/core-ui` `./pager` raised from 2.8 to 4.1 (measured 2.70 before, 4.06
   now), above spec 0023 §1's 2 KB cap. This is PC-128's raise (D-371), already reviewed.
3. The pager asks for idle itself rather than through the block's `on idle` trigger, whose
   scheduler put +0.56 KB on Acme's initialJs (PC-128's measure).

### Open risks

- The stand-in copies the select trigger's chrome in its own rules. A later change to the
  trigger's look must follow in `tm-pager.css`; the unit test that compares their computed
  chrome at sm, md and lg catches a drift.
- Showcase `pager.spec.ts` measures `tm-select`'s box after `appStable`. It passes because the
  idle load lands before the stability hook settles. If that stops holding, the box test needs
  to wait for the select.
- PC-128 will later merge this identical pager change under its own (D-389). Its files equal
  this branch's apart from the Lucide import form, so the merge should resolve to platform-core's
  side.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- That the pager files equal PC-128's reviewed versions (`git diff e7f53338 -- <pager paths>`
  shows only the Lucide import lines; `git diff 94b802aa -- support/acme.ts` shows nothing).
- That nothing search-specific came over, and that leaving out tm-select-base's
  `preventDefault()` changes no pager behaviour here.
- The handover's inert pass, the press guard and the replay order, as before.
