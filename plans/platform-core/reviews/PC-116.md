# PC-116: /shell entry point: frame, tenantless shell, tenant picker, signed-out page

## Implementer notes

### What was built

The shared shell frame, the tenantless shell and its two pages, their page contexts and a harness,
all in `@tellma/core/shell` (spec 0027 §1, §5.2–§5.4, §6, §14).

- **Frame** — `client/projects/core/tellma-core/shell/src/frame/tm-shell-frame.ts`.
  - `tm-shell-frame`: the skip link (first focusable, shown on focus, moves focus to `main`
    without a router navigation), a `header` with `[tmShellBar]`, `tm-banner-host` and
    `[tmShellPinnedBar]` rows, `main#tm-main` (the one scrolling region, stable gutter), and an
    empty hidden `aside`. Print hides the chrome; nothing animates.
  - An immersive frame (`TmShell.immersive`) renders no `header`.
  - A root `TmShellRowsWriter` writes the newest live frame's `--tm-app-bar-height`,
    `--tm-banner-height` and `--tm-pinned-bar-height` on the root element, so two frames that
    overlap during a navigation never fight, and removes them when the last frame ends. The grid
    and the defaults stay in `styles/tm-frame.css`, as before.
  - The frame registers `tm.shell.skip` and `tm.shell.tenants` (the picker route's document
    title, which the title strategy reads before the picker's chunk loads).
- **Tenantless pages, a lazy chunk of `/shell`** — `shell/src/tenantless/`.
  - `tm-tenantless.ts` is the chunk's entry (`tellma-core-shell-tm-tenantless-*.mjs`, budgeted at
    8 KB in `lazyChunkBudgetsInKb`); `tm-shell-components.ts` maps the route names to loaders.
  - `tm-tenantless-shell.ts`: the 40 px ink bar: wordmark (mark below `md`) linked to `/`, the
    language menu (only with two or more languages; globe below `md`; switching sends no
    request), the account menu (name over email, avatar below `md`; My account to
    `identity.accountUrl` with `return_url` and `ui_locales`, only while the distribution
    document holds it; Sign out). `signedOut` shows the wordmark alone.
  - `tm-tenant-rows.ts`: pure row model. Name slot by language subtag (D-269 keyof typing on
    `NAME_SLOTS`), live tenants by shown name in the active language's collation with their
    sandboxes nested beneath, unattached sandboxes last, retired tenants dropped, badges and
    unavailable reasons (removed > suspended > provisioning), search over every name folded by
    `tmSearchNormalize` and the id exactly, dimmed live parents of matching sandboxes.
  - `tm-tenant-picker.ts`: stored list in the first frame, `revalidateUser()` in
    `afterNextRender` (no request before the first render — the held PC-114 stand-in's rule
    kept); skeleton (four rows) while nothing is stored; the error state, from a lazily imported
    `@tellma/core/errors`, when the read fails with nothing stored, with Try again; the empty
    state with Sign out; search from seven memberships with `?q=` (replaceUrl), Enter following
    the only enabled match, Mod+K (`tm.launcher.open`) focusing it; the no-match line; the
    tenant-not-found notice (named, or by id when the list no longer holds it) with Dismiss
    dropping `notice` and `from`; Connect links to `TmUrls.forTenant(id).home()` with preload on
    hover and focus, or a disabled button with its reason; `tm.shell.picker-shown` once; the
    `/tenants` custom page context (count, query, the Name/Id rows shown, up to 200).
  - `tm-signed-out-page.ts`: no API call of its own, Sign in to `/`, and with a profile cookie
    "signed in as" plus Continue; `/signed-out` custom page context.
  - Strings: `tm-tenantless-strings.ts` (`tm.tenantless.*`) and the Arabic pack's mirror.
- **Core changes**
  - `TmShellComponents` members are loaders (`() => Promise<Type>`); `tmShellComponent` calls them.
  - `TmLaunchCache.revalidateUser()` settles with the tenants read's `TmApiError` or `null`.
  - `/tenants` route title key `tm.shell.tenants`; `tm.shell.signedOut.signIn` kept in English
    for the error state and the session flows.
- **Build tooling** — `client/projects/core/tellma-core-tools/src/output/preloads.ts` and
  `stats.ts`: the `/shell` and `/attention` modulepreloads now start at the chunk holding each
  entry point's starting module (`tmEntryPointRoot`: the built bundle, or `public-api.ts` in a
  workspace) instead of every chunk holding any of its sources. Before, a workspace build
  (Acme, the fixture app) modulepreloaded the entry points' own lazy chunks: the tenantless
  chunk took the fixture's initial JavaScript to 248 KB, over the 230 KB warn level, and
  `/attention`'s server-call and arrival-toast chunks were preloaded too.
- **Harness** — `client/projects/core/tellma-core-testing/src/tm-tenant-picker-harness.ts`:
  `TmTenantPickerHarness` and `TmTenantPickerRowHarness`, exported from `@tellma/core-testing`.
- **Tests** — `tm-tenant-rows.spec.ts` (unit, name slots, order, collation, badges, search),
  `tm-tenant-picker.spec.ts` (harness, every bullet of the checkpoint's test list),
  `tm-tenantless-shell.spec.ts` (bar and signed-out page), `frame/tm-shell-frame.spec.ts`.
  The lazy-chunk specs wait with `tmSettleUntil` and passed with a 300 ms delay injected in the
  tenantless import and the errors import (reverted). Two existing specs were adjusted:
  `tm-route-host.spec.ts` (new signed-out heading, waits for the chunk) and
  `tm-error-presenter.spec.ts` (filters the idle other-sessions sweep, which can land while the
  picker's chunk loads).
- **Docs** — `@tellma/core` README (`/shell` row, frame and tenantless paragraph),
  `@tellma/core-testing` README, ARCHITECTURE.md's `@tellma/core` row.

### Deviations

1. The tenantless pages are a lazy chunk of `/shell`, so `TmTenantPicker`, `TmSignedOutPage` and
   `TmTenantlessShell` are exported as types only (spec 0027 §1 lists them as `/shell` exports).
   Reason: they keep `/search-normalize` out of `/shell`'s static graph and out of every tenant
   first frame, within the 1.35 KB budget's spirit.
2. `TmLaunchCache.revalidateUser()` returns `Promise<TmApiError | null>` rather than
   `Promise<void>` (spec 0025 §3.1), so the picker can tell a failed read from a stored list.
3. The tenant-shell stand-in renders inside the frame with `appBar=false`, so the tenant layout is
   now the 100dvh grid with only `main` scrolling.
4. The picker's title string is `tm.shell.tenants` in the frame's scope, not in the chunk's.
5. The modulepreload rule changed (see Build tooling): `/attention`'s lazy chunks are no longer
   preloaded in a workspace build either, which the spec already called lazy.
6. Budgets: `"."` 49.89 → 49.9 (51,091 B against the 51,087 B ceiling, +4 B for the route
   title, the loader map and the `revalidateUser` result; cap line 49.79 of 50 at gzip 9);
   `./shell` 1.35 → 2.35 (1.32 → 2.00 KB, +0.68 KB, rounded up to the next 0.5 KB per D-031)
   with the 8 KB `tm-tenantless` lazy budget (7.69 KB); `locale-ar` `./platform-strings`
   3 → 3.5 (+0.51 KB of Arabic strings).

### Open risks

- The fixture app's initial JavaScript (with the preloaded `/shell` and `/attention`) now sits
  just under the 230 KB warn level of `tellma-client build` (it was 230.01 KB before the frame's
  row rules were trimmed); Acme measures 230.41 KB, a warning only (its build already reports
  warnings). The base's figure was not measured, so the frame's share is an estimate (about
  1.2 KB of `tm-shell-frame` against the 2 KB or so of `/attention` lazy chunks no longer
  preloaded). PC-118's
  tenant shell will cross the warn level on the fixture's zero-warning build test unless that
  checkpoint takes savings or the level is revisited.

- PC-114 (held) expects the old copy: "Choose a company", "You're signed out", Connect links
  named after the tenant. The spec's copy ("Choose a tenant", "You signed out", "Connect to
  {name}") is what is built; PC-114's e2e needs those strings at merge.
- PC-114's fix (D-270) memoizes the route loader in `tmShellComponent`; the merge must memoize the
  loader's resolved class now that each member is a loader.
- The tenant layout change (deviation 3) may move PC-114's tenant e2e geometry.
- No `./shell` entry in `ENTRY_POINT_CAPS`: PC-112 (held) moves caps to `"tellma".capsInKb`, and
  the 2.35 KB budget sits far under the spec's 16 KB.
- The Arabic platform strings budget will need about 4 KB when PC-125's strings land.
