### Deviations

1. Spec 0029 §1 places the editor and the picker in `/entity`; they are lazy entry points of their
   own (orchestrator instruction: `/entity` was at 24.96 of 25). `tmQxParameters` and `tmQxInsert`
   moved with the editor. §1's done-when of about 28 KB for §6 and §8–§14 is replaced, not met:
   `/entity` 24.55 + the editor 7.97 + the picker 4.56 = 37.08 KB, and 41.07 KB with
   `/column-filter` (3.99), which §1 also places in `/entity` (37.01 and 41.0 before the review
   fixes). Each part has its own budget (25, 8 and 5). Flagged for Ahmad: §1 should list the
   three entry points and their sizes.
2. The `/entity` ceiling is not lowered: 24.55 rounds up to 25, the current ceiling.
3. The Gregorian label is `tm.pathPicker.gregorian`, not `tm.entity.datePartGregorian`:
   `ɵregisterStrings` registers a scope once, and `entity` belongs to `TmEntityActions`, so a
   second registration under it would be dropped.
4. In `columns` mode a multilingual group's children are its twins only (`Name2`, `Name3`). The
   reason is §6.1's "No slot-1 key": the group's own key is the column key of the active slot,
   so there is no key for slot 1 to give a child. This contradicts §11.2's example children
   `Name (E)`, `Name (ع)`, a spec misalignment flagged for Ahmad. In `fields` mode the group is a
   leaf (Queryex reads `Name`).
5. "(Gregorian)" is added only to the parts that take a calendar (Year, Quarter, Month, Quarter of
   year, Month of year). Week, Day and Day of week are the same in every calendar.
6. The lexical result type is `TmQxParameterSites`, since §10.1's `TmQxParameter` is the draft's
   merged parameter.
7. Undo of a re-spaced drop takes two steps (the spaces, then the drop). `insertAtCaret` is one
   step, and focuses the editor, because a native undoable edit needs focus.
8. `accept` hides refused leaves; a refused branch still shows (it may hold accepted fields) but
   neither drags, activates nor selects.
9. The draft drops confirmed types the server refuses (`HierarchyId`, `Geography`) and repeats of
   a name, client-side.
10. The Arabic is in a new `@tellma/locale-ar/queryex-strings` entry point that `tmArabic()` loads
    with the other two on the first switch to Arabic, not with the editor. In `./platform-strings`
    it measured 4.01 against 4 after two raises. A part that loads with the editor needs a
    `TmLocalization` seam in the primary, which may not grow (49.89 of 49.89).
11. Spec §10.4's three-engine e2e of undo is not added: no page hosts the editor before specs
    0030 and 0032. A Chromium unit test pins the single undo step.
12. `TmPathPicker.text(node)` is public, beyond §11.1's contract. A navigation's Queryex text is
    its foreign-key path, which `TmPathNode` does not carry, and §11.3's path without a drag
    (`activate` then `insertAtCaret`) needs that text. Flagged for Ahmad: §11.1 should gain it,
    since specs 0030 and 0034 will call it.

### Open risks

- The mirror and the textarea must keep identical metrics. They share every font, padding,
  wrapping and scrollbar-gutter rule, and the scroll is mirrored. Parameters are bold in the
  mirror only, which is safe in a monospace face but would drift in a proportional fallback.
- `document.execCommand('insertText')` is deprecated but remains the only undoable script edit;
  the fallback (`setRangeText`) works but is not undoable.
- `TmFakeDiscover` folds names with `toUpperCase`, close to but not exactly the engine's fold.
- The search walks up to three levels of every navigation. On a wide model this builds many
  nodes on the first keystroke (each cached), bounded by the 200-match cap only once that is
  reached.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- The draft's stale-answer and abort logic (`#request` equality, `#schedule`, `#check`) and the
  online-retry effect.
- `#respaceDrop`'s diff and single replacement, and the `#editing` guard around native edits.
- The tree's node rules against §11.2, especially the twins, blobs, reachability and date parts.
- The re-split of the Arabic table (deviation 10) and the missing Arabic-loads-with-the-editor
  seam.

**R1-1 (minor): the draft applies an answer checked against another root, aggregation or set of confirmed types**

- **Where:** `client/projects/core/tellma-core/expression-editor/src/tm-queryex-draft.ts:184`,
  `:252-272` and `:317-341`.
- **The rule as built:** `#answer` is kept and applied whenever a clause's text equals the text
  that was sent (`answer.sent.get(wire) === text`). Nothing checks that the request's other parts
  still hold: root, aggregation, profile and confirmed types.
- **Failure scenario:**
  1. The report studio's draft has `filter = "Code = 1 and Accnt.Code = 'x'"` on root
     `acme.SalesInvoiceLine`. It is checked with a QX2001 on `Accnt`.
  2. The author switches the root to `gl.Center`.
  3. For at least 250 ms plus the round trip, the filter keeps the old root's wavy underline,
     status line and `aria-invalid`. `paths()` and the parameters' types and entities still
     describe the old root.
  4. If the new request is `null`, for example after the root is cleared, the stale answer stays
     applied indefinitely while the status reads idle.
- **Fix:** keep the request's non-text identity with the applied answer: root, aggregate, profile
  and the confirmed parameters (the parts of `#request`'s key other than the texts). Apply an
  answer only while that identity equals the current request's. Alternatively, clear `#answer` in
  `#schedule` when it changes or when the request becomes null.
- **Test:** a checked answer with a problem; change `root`; expect the server problem gone at once
  and `paths()` empty.

**R1-2 (minor): no test fails if a change stops aborting the call in flight**

- **Where:** `tm-queryex-draft.spec.ts:99-115` and `tm-expression-editor.spec.ts:215-227`.
- **The gap:** both "abort" tests hold two calls, release them, and assert only the final
  diagnostics and the call count. The final diagnostics are already hidden by the stale-text
  rule. So the tests stay green if you delete `this.#controller?.abort()` from
  `tm-queryex-draft.ts:387`, or if `#check` stops passing `{ signal }`.
- **What that hides:** without the abort, the first answer lands and sets the status to `checked`
  while the second call is still in flight. That breaks spec 0029 §10.2's abort rule and §18's
  "two aborted, one applied".
- **Fix:** assert `TmFakeApi.calls()[0].options.signal?.aborted === true` after the second change.
  Then release the held answers one at a time and assert that the status stays `checking` until
  the second answer resolves.

**R1-3 (minor): a drop just before text that begins with the dropped text is re-spaced to the wrong side**

- **Where:** `tm-expression-editor.ts:665-680`.
- **The cause:** `#respaceDrop` finds the dropped run by the longest common head of the texts
  before and after the drop. When the dropped text repeats the start of what follows the drop
  point, the head runs past the real drop point.
- **Failure scenario:**
  1. The value is `Name2 = 'x'`. The user drags `Name` and drops it at offset 0.
  2. The browser's text becomes `NameName2 = 'x'`.
  3. The head is 4, so the dropped run is read as offset 4..8, and `ɵtmQxPlanInsert(base, 4,
     'Name')` moves the caret out of `Name2`.
  4. The result is `Name2 Name = 'x'`. The user expects `Name Name2 = 'x'`, which is what
     `tmQxInsert(before, 0, 'Name')` gives.

  `Code` dropped before `Codex` fails the same way. The same ambiguity can also shift the tint
  and the selection.
- **Fix:** take the dropped run from the textarea's selection after the drop, where the engine
  leaves the dropped text selected. Use it when `selectionEnd - selectionStart` equals the length
  gained, and fall back to the diff otherwise. Add a unit case for a drop at 0 before `Name2`.
  PC-168's three-engine e2e should confirm each engine's post-drop selection.

### Responses

- **R1-1: fixed.**
  - Each request now carries a context beside its texts: the root and, for a query, the
    aggregation and the parameter types it sends. The profile is fixed per draft.
  - The applied answer keeps its context. A computed `#applied` gives the answer only while
    there is a request and its context equals the answer's. `diagnostics`, `parameters` and
    `paths` read `#applied`, so a change of root, aggregation or confirmed types, or a request
    that becomes `null`, drops the answer at once.
  - New spec: "drops an answer once the root, the aggregation or the confirmed types change, or
    nothing is left to check". It covers all four cases and fails when `#applied` returns the
    raw answer.
- **R1-2: fixed.**
  - New spec: "aborts the call in flight, and stays checking until the latest answer lands".
  - Each answer waits on its own release. The spec asserts that the first call's signal is
    aborted after the second change. It then releases the first answer and asserts the status
    is still `checking`, and finally releases the second.
  - It fails when `this.#controller?.abort()` is removed.
- **R1-3: fixed.**
  - `#respaceDrop` takes the dropped run from the textarea's selection after the drop when the
    text without that selection is the text before the drop. Otherwise it falls back to the
    head/tail diff.
  - New spec: "re-spaces a drop by the selection the browser leaves, when the drop repeats the
    text after it". It drops `Name` at 0 before `Name2 = 'x'`, then expects `Name Name2 = 'x'`
    with [0, 4] selected. It fails without the selection branch.
  - PC-168's e2e should confirm each engine's post-drop selection (R1-6 carry).
- **R1-4: fixed.**
  - Picker: "drags a tm.path payload of the node, named by its slash-joined path label". It
    checks the payload's kind `tm.path`, its `data` node, and `pathLabel()` and `label`:
    `Currency / Code`, and `Due date / Month of year` for a date part.
  - Picker: "draws each value type's glyph, and each other kind's". It checks the drawn SVG of
