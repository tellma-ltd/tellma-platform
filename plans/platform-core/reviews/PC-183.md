# PC-183: Audience resolver

## Implementer notes

### What was built

- **The contract** (`src/core/Tellma.Core.Abstractions/Sharing`): `IAudienceResolver`,
  `AudienceQuery`, `ShareAudience`, `AudienceMember`, `AudienceReason`, `AudienceGrant`,
  `AudienceReasonKind`, `AudienceAccess`, and `SharingTelemetryNames`
  (`tellma.sharing.audience.members`, tag `resource`). The shapes follow spec 0033 §6.1. The
  resolver also takes a `CancellationToken`.
- **`AudienceResolver<TUser, TRole>`** (`src/core/Tellma.Core/Sharing/AudienceResolver.cs`) is
  scoped and closed over the leaves in `SharingComposition`. `ShareableShapes` (a singleton map
  from resource to shape) and `SharingMetrics` are registered beside it.
  - Round trip 1 is one guarded Read batch. It holds:
    - the row under the caller's `Share` decision filter, as a `Rows` query over a key-set;
    - per group (the row itself, and the rows seen through): a statement with the rows and their
      owners, the grants (with the grantee's audience/active/public flags), and the stored
      permissions of active roles on the resource, its owner chain or its aliases, or on `*` with
      the registered actions. It also fills batch-local id tables for people, candidates, roles
      and the "reaches everyone" marker;
    - a common statement with the current audience memberships of the named non-public roles,
      the audience size, and `TOP (MaxAudienceMembers)` users by display name in the reader's
      content language (only when a grant may reach everyone and the members are visible);
    - per data securable (skipped when a bespoke `Read`/`*`/own-action criterion exists on any
      level): the reader roles, the public-role Everyone bit, the exact `COUNT(DISTINCT)` of
      audience readers, and which named candidates read it;
    - the `core.User` (plus `Kind`) and `core.Role` related projections, and the projection of
      the rows seen through under the caller's `Read` filter.
  - Permissions are classified with `PermissionClassifier` and matched with
    `AccessComposition.Matches` against the shareable's `Read` securable, as the permission-set
    build does. Edit comes from `Save` or `*`; every other matching action gives View.
  - Round trip 2 runs only when a matched permission is filtered. It is one `Rows` query per
    distinct (group, filter text, version), `FilterTree.Leaf(text, version)` over a key-set of
    the group's ids. A filter beyond the compile ceilings counts as unsatisfied.
- **`AudienceComposer`** (`src/core/Tellma.Core/Sharing/AudienceComposer.cs`) is the pure merge:
  - one grant per owner, user share, role share, Everyone share and stored permission that
    reaches the row, plus one `Through` grant per row seen through (the union of its own grants);
  - `Total` and `CannotRead` are exact. A grant reaching everyone counts from the audience size
    and the readers' count, and is never expanded into ids;
  - members carry every reason and the highest access, are sorted by display name in the reader's
    culture, and are cut at the cap. `Members` is null and `Capped` false without
    `core.Role` × `Read`.
- **`ReportService.AudienceAsync`** (`[ApiAction("audience", Action = "Share", Mutation = false,
  Idempotent = true, Long = true)]`):
  - reads the report's `Root` through its own pipeline query (not found when invisible);
  - asks `IDashboardReadThrough.DashboardsUsing([id])` and takes the distinct ids, the first 20
    by id;
  - resolves `("core.Report", id, [(Root, Read)], "core.Dashboard", ids)`.
  The route is in the route and stack goldens, the client entity fixture, and the Acme
  `web.json`/`contracts.json` snapshots. It is driven end to end in `ReportOperationTests`
  and listed in `ProjectedRouteTests`. Its label `Core_Report_Action_Audience` is in both
  `Strings` resources.
- **Tests:**
  - `AudienceComposerTests` (9 unit tests).
  - `ReportAudienceTests` (9 integration tests on real SQL): CannotRead per grant and per member;
    the report route with the bespoke self criterion on `core.User`; a service account via a
    shared role, a public role and a permission role; a deactivated grantee with `Members: 0`;
    hidden members plus a 404 for a grantee without `Share`; the cap with an exact `Total`;
    600 permission holders counted exactly, plus the metric; a filtered permission counted only
    when the row satisfies it; Through.
  - `ReportOperationTests` now reads the audience over HTTP and checks §6.2's example grant shape.

### Deviations

1. **Round trips.** §6.2 puts the per-filter counts in the first round trip. A permission's filter
   is Queryex text that only C# compiles, so it cannot be compiled before the permissions are
   read. Round trip 1 therefore carries everything else, the Through grants included (their ids
   are known up front). Round trip 2 runs only when a matched permission is filtered. The result
   is 1 trip without filtered permissions and 2 with them, against the spec's 1, or 2 with
   Through.
2. **`ReportService.AudienceAsync` reads the root first** (one more round trip, through the
   pipeline under the caller's `Read`). The data securable has to be known before the resolver
   composes. So a caller who may share but not read the report gets a 404.
3. **The rows seen through are projected only when the caller's `Read` on `ThroughResource`
   admits them** (a privacy choice). The spec names the projection but no authorization. Their
   grants count regardless.
4. **Bespoke criteria count for everyone on the actions `Read`, `*` or the data securable's own
   action.** The spec says `Read` or `*`. A wildcard-resource permission counts only for the
   actions the levels register, plus `Read` and `*`, as the holders resolver does. A public role's
   wildcard-resource permission is not an Everyone reader.
5. **Unsatisfied filtered permissions are left out of `Grants`.** A permission whose filter drifted
   (a classifier problem), or that is filtered on a securable with no filter root, is skipped.
6. **`ThroughIds` are de-duplicated, non-positive ids and the row itself are dropped**, and an
   unregistered `ThroughResource` is accepted when there are no ids. `core.Dashboard` is not
   shareable yet, and `NullDashboardReadThrough` answers nothing.
7. **The users' projection in `Related` is not narrowed by the caller's `core.User` × `Read`**
   (the details-page principle). `Kind` is added to the projection.
8. **The membership predicate is written literally** in the statements, in the place of
   `AccessFilters.CurrentMembershipSql("m")`. A text hole binds as a parameter, as in
   `AccessHoldersResolver`.
9. **`IAudienceResolver.ResolveAsync` takes an optional `CancellationToken`**, and
   `AudienceAsync` takes one as well.

### Open risks

- **`me()` in a permission filter evaluates as the caller** in round trip 2, not as each holder.
  A permission like `OwnerId = me()` therefore counts its role's members for any row the
  caller owns. The spec's "one count per distinct filter text" implies this. A per-holder
  evaluation would need one statement per holder.
- The members listed when a grant reaches everyone are the first `MaxAudienceMembers` by the
  database collation's order (`COALESCE(NULLIF(<twin>, ''), Name), Id`). They are then sorted in
  C# by culture compare. The two orders can disagree at the cut-off.
- The class-level integration database is shared by the tests of the class. They avoid
  cross-talk by granting roles through SQL (a `Publish` permission would reach every report)
  and by filtering every seeded report permission to its one report.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- Whether the round-trip-1 SQL counts exactly: audience users only (active, `Kind <> 'System'`),
  current memberships only, inactive roles excluded, public roles reaching everyone. The
  `candidates` table must hold every user a reach can name before the per-securable reader
  statements run (statement order in the batch).
- The permission matching (`Classify` plus `Matches` on `Read`), the Edit mapping, and the
  wildcard handling against spec 0014 §5.2 and §5.5.
- `CannotRead` for an everyone reach (`Audience − Readers.Count`) against the explicit-reach path.
- The authorization: the `Share` filter on the row, the `core.Role` × `Read` gate on `Members`,
  and the `Read` gate on the rows seen through.
