# PC-151: Cross-cutting accessibility and stability gates

## Implementer notes

### What was built

Shared Playwright batteries over every spec 0023 story, driven by one registry, plus the docs
verification the DoD's Placement bullet asks for.

- **Registry** — `client/e2e/support/additions-stories.ts`.
  - `ADDITIONS_ENTRY_POINTS` (the thirty §1 entry points) and `ADDITIONS_STORIES` (32 stories:
    the thirty, plus `tiles-tree` and `tiles-large`).
  - Each story has a `ready` wait, the extra `states` the axe battery opens, and a keydown `probe`
    or `{ none: reason }`.
  - The file's header comment maps every §13 bullet to its pinning spec (copied below).
- **Support helpers** (new).
  - `e2e/support/paint-probe.ts` — the keydown-to-paint probe.
    - A rAF loop registered a frame ahead resizes an off-screen sentinel on the first frame after
      the key. The sentinel's ResizeObserver runs after that frame's rAF callbacks and layout,
      before paint, and checks the DOM for the feedback (`PaintedFeedback`: selector, plus count,
      text, contains, class or value).
    - The verdict is an ordering inside one frame, not a duration, so a loaded runner cannot fail
      it.
    - Arming fails if the feedback already shows. The result waits until the feedback shows at
      all, so a dead key and a late key fail with different messages.
    - Space on a native control triggers on `keyup` (`trigger: 'keyup'`).
  - `e2e/support/pixels.ts` — decodes a clipped screenshot inside the page (createImageBitmap and
    OffscreenCanvas, no image library), and resolves system colours to the forced palette
    (`paintedColour`).
  - `e2e/support/motion.ts` — an init-script recorder of every `animationstart` and
    `transitionrun`.
    - Anything changing only appearance in place is classed as not motion: colours, `box-shadow`
      (the focus glow) and `visibility`.
    - Keyframe properties come from `getAnimations()`. An animation whose keyframes can't be read
      counts as motion, which is the safe side.
    - `ALLOWED_MOTION` lists what may run, each entry with its reason.
- **Batteries** (new specs in `client/e2e/specs/`).
  - `additions-axe.spec.ts`
    - **Registry checks.** Every entry point is a library folder with a battery story, and every
      registry story is a showcase story.

      - date-range and date-time fields in error;
      - tiles busy and custom-empty;
      - the drop target hovered;
      - the `tiles-large` floor.
    - **Combinations.** Each state is scanned in light/dark × LTR/RTL: one rotating combination
      in the PR tier, the other three `@nightly` (see Deviations).
  - `additions-keydown-paint.spec.ts`
    - One probe for each of the 28 stories with a key. Four stories have none, each with a
      recorded reason: avatar, skeleton, relative-time and description-list.
    - The PR tier asserts that the feedback is in the first painted frame.
    - A `@perf` twin also asserts that the frame is laid out at most 50 ms after the keydown.
  - `additions-motion.spec.ts`
    - Each of the 32 stories is loaded, put through every state and its probe key, each step from
      a fresh load.
    - Under `reduce`, zero motion records are allowed. Without the preference, only
      `ALLOWED_MOTION` may run.
    - Ten pins prove that the recorder sees each allowed §2.2 motion without the preference and
      sees nothing of it under `reduce`: the knob, the sweep, the ring, the modal fade, and the

  - `additions-forced-colors.spec.ts` — proofs from painted pixels, not computed styles:

### Deviations

1. **The PR tier scans each battery state in one theme×dir combination, rotating; the other three
   are `@nightly`.** All four run nightly. This follows PLAN §6's "axe smoke subset" in PR, and
   keeps about 40 scans in PR instead of about 160. Each story spec's own as-loaded floor still
   runs in all four combinations in PR.
2. **`ALLOWED_MOTION` goes beyond §2.2's four motions.** It adds the motion of the older components
   the stories compose: the spinner spin, the tooltip and popover entrances, and the select caret
   turn. All of them are static under `reduce`, which the battery asserts.
3. **The motion recorder counts colour, `box-shadow` and `visibility` changes as not motion.**
   form-field and select fade a focus glow with no reduce block. That is appearance in place, not
   movement.
4. **Resolved sizes are measured with `setViewportSize(390)`**, in Chromium and in the Pixel 7
   project, since the Pixel 7 descriptor itself is 412 px wide.
5. **Tracked exemption: every host of the date picker's popup stops at the popup's open state.**
   This covers the date range's and the date-time field's Alt+ArrowDown (Review 1 added the
   date-time probe). Each probe checks that the combobox reports `aria-expanded="true"` in the
   key's first frame. The calendar itself renders a frame or more later, because the date
   picker defers the popup's views until first open (spec 0005 §16, an `@defer (on immediate)`
   block). Fixing that would mean un-deferring an older component's popup chunk, which is
   outside spec 0023 and against spec 0005's own budget decision. The registry comments record
   the exemption on both probes, and the axe battery's "calendar open" state still checks the
   calendar once it shows.
6. **Root services now appear in components.json, llms.txt and MCP** (schema 3.0.0, with
   `members`, `kind: 'service'` and a nullable `selector`). This goes beyond what spec 0023 states,
   to make "MCP entries for every entry point" true.
7. **The `@perf` twin's 50 ms budget carries a CI multiplier** (Review 1). It is
   `TM_PERF_MULTIPLIER` when set, else 2 under `CI`, else 1, since PLAN §6 names a multiplier
   but the repo had none yet. The span runs from the event the control acts on (keyup for the
   switch), not from the keydown.

### Open risks

- **The probe's ResizeObserver timing.** Chromium delivers RO notifications after layout and
  before paint in the same frame (the HTML event loop's "update the rendering" steps). Firefox and
  WebKit are not exercised: the battery is not tagged `@cross-engine`.
- **Forced-colors pixel proofs depend on Chromium's emulated palette.** Highlight must differ from
  Canvas and CanvasText. Thresholds are a share of the ring or area, not exact counts.
- **The motion battery's CDK 1 ms backdrop transitions.** If CDK's transparent-backdrop opacity
  transition shows up, it is a recorder classification question, not component motion.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- `paint-probe.ts`: whether the frame-ordering argument holds, and whether a feedback check could
  pass on a stale DOM.
- The registry's probes: each `painted` check must be the feedback of *that* key, not an
  incidental DOM change.
- The extractor's member filter (interface-contract exclusion), and the schema bump's effect on
  consumers of components.json (only the MCP server and llms.txt).

**R1-1 (major). The switch's keydown-to-paint probe pins the browser's native checkbox, not the
switch.**
- **Where:** `client/e2e/support/additions-stories.ts:794` uses
  `painted: { selector: '[data-testid="switch-notify"] input:checked', count: 1 }`.
- **The problem:** the browser flips a native checkbox on the Space keyup by itself. What paints
  the switch (track fill and knob position) is the host class `.tm-switch--checked`, bound from
  the `checked` model (`tm-switch.ts:76`, `tm-switch.css:88-93`). The probe therefore passes even
  when the component writes its model late, or never.
- **Shown:** I deferred the switch's own `change`/`click`/`input` listeners by 100 ms. The probe
  stayed green, and the host still lacked `tm-switch--checked` when the probe returned.
- **Fix:** probe the host class:
  `painted: { selector: '[data-testid="switch-notify"]', className: 'tm-switch--checked' }`.
  (`data-testid` sits on the `tm-switch` host.) Keep `trigger: 'keyup'`.

**R1-2 (major). library-docs.spec's harness check is vacuous for every component and directive.**
- **Where:** `client/tools/tests/library-docs.spec.mts:126` matches
  `${entry.name}Harness === harness`. For a component or directive, `entry.name` is its
  selector, not its class (`tm-copy-button`, `ng-template[tmTreeNode]`), so the match only ever
  fires for services. The `DocEntry.name` TSDoc at line 18 ("The class name") is wrong for the
  same reason.
- **Effect:** of the 26 §1 harnesses, only TmBusyOverlayHarness, TmConfirmHarness and
  TmErrorDialogHarness are checked against an entry. mcp-server.spec separately checks
  TmToastHarness. If `harnessOf` stopped pairing component harnesses (returned `null`), the
  "names each additions harness, on the entry it drives" test would stay green. That check is the
  "final llms.txt/MCP entry verification" deliverable for harnesses.
- **Fix:** for each listed harness, assert that some entry of that entry point has
  `entry.harness === harness`, for example
  `expect(documented.some((e) => e.harness === harness)).toBe(true)`. Correct the TSDoc.

**R1-3 (major). The sizes battery leaves out two of §2.2's sized controls, with no deviation
recorded.**
- **Where:** `client/e2e/specs/additions-sizes.spec.ts:43-108`.
- **The gap:** §2.2 puts `search-field` and `copy` (through `tmButton`) on the size ladder at the
  resolved size, and §12's touch row asks for "resolved sizes at 390 px". The battery covers eight
  controls and drops both. touch-size.spec covers only the plain `tm-form-field`, and no other
  spec pins `tm-search-field`'s or a copy button's size at 390 or 768 px.
- **Today's behaviour is right:** I measured both in a scratch run. The search field's box is 46
  px with `tm-form-field--lg` at 390 px, and 30 px with `--sm` at 768 px. The labelled copy
  button is 46 px with `tm-button--lg`, and 30 px with `--sm`. So this is a missing gate, not a
  bug. PLAN §7.1 item 1 still requires the omission to be pinned or recorded.
- **Fix:** add two entries:
  - `search-field`: `search-main`'s first `tm-form-field`, reporting
    `{ classPrefix: 'tm-form-field' }`, with FIELD heights;
  - `copy`: `copy-label`'s button, reporting `{ classPrefix: 'tm-button' }`, at 46 and 30 px.

  Otherwise record why they are left out.

**R1-4 (minor). The date-picker exemption is narrower than the behaviour it exempts.**

**R1-5 (minor). Nothing forces a new additions story or entry point into the registry, though the

**R1-6 (minor). The `@perf` twin times keyup-triggered probes from the keydown.**

**R1-7 (minor). The new `members` field advertises internal wiring as API, and files an output
