# PC-118: Tenant shell, app bar, markers, session menu, About and Shortcuts dialogs

## Implementer notes

### What was built

All paths are under `client/projects/core/` unless stated.

- **The tenant shell** (`tellma-core/shell/src/tm-tenant-shell.ts`) replaces the stand-in. It
  renders the frame with the app bar in its first row (the frame's app-bar row is now 40 px in the
  tenant shell too) and, in `main`, one of three things:
  - the route host;
  - a failed cold sync's `tm-error-state`, from `TmErrorPresenter.describe(error, { kind: 'read' })`
    with Retry re-running `TmLaunchCache.ɵenter`. This includes `client-outdated`, through the
    error views' own view. When the error views fail to load, or the failure is not a `TmApiError`,
    the shell shows its own line with Try again;
  - `TmShellMain.override`.

  The failures that other code answers (`cancelled`, `unauthenticated`, `tenant-not-found`,
  `tenant-unavailable`) show nothing here. The shell registers `Mod+/` (the Shortcuts dialog) and
  keeps starting `TmArrivals`. The error views' loader moved to `tm-shell-errors.ts`
  (`TM_SHELL_ERROR_VIEWS`) and is shared with the tenant picker.
- **The app bar** (`shell/src/app-bar/tm-app-bar.ts`) shows the following. The §7.1 breakpoints are
  CSS media queries, so the first frame is right at every width.
  - The wordmark, with the mark below md.
  - Menu and search, grouped from md, with the field from lg and the `Ctrl K`/`⌘ K` keycap from xl.
  - Back and Forward in installed mode (`tm-installed-history.ts`). They use the Navigation API, and
    without it the router's `ɵrouterPageId` stands in for the depth counter.
  - The slots, from `TM_APP_DECLARATIONS.appBar` (`tm-shell-slots.ts`). Each badge is called once
    in the shell's injector, and its `tm-counter` is overlaid on the button.
  - Fixed 30 px boxes for the bell and the tray, and zero-width hidden boxes for `help` and
    `assistant`.
  - The markers, as chips from md and 24 px circles below.
  - The session button: the initials on the `core.User:{id}` tone, then the names by width, with a
    96 px skeleton for a cold tenant name. Its accessible name carries one suffix per marker.

  Who and where the user is lives in `TmShellIdentity`, which the bar, the menu and the popovers
  share.
- **The overlays chunk** (`shell/src/session/tm-session-panels.ts`, loaded by `TmShellOverlays`) is
  the one lazy host for:
  - the session menu (`tm-session-menu.ts`);
  - the 280 px marker popover, with the live tenant's "Open {name}" link;
  - the slot popover (size md, from lg up) or an end `TmModal` drawer (below lg, and from the menu's
    slot rows);
  - the About and Shortcuts dialogs, each a deferred chunk. They open at once through
    `TmShellLazyContent`, a placeholder in the dialog's height.

  The bar attaches the host after its first render and loads the chunk at idle, or on the first
  hover, focus or press of a button. A press before the load runs its action once the chunk lands.
  `TmShellOverlays.run` acts synchronously once loaded, so overlays open in the gesture's frame.
- **The session menu rows** follow the §10.1 table: the header (`tm-avatar` 28 px, name, email), the
  tenant card, My settings, Language and Calendar with two or more choices, Theme (radios,
  `keepOpen`), Keyboard shortcuts, About with `TmAppVersion.display`, the slots below md, and Sign
  out after a separator.
- **About** (`shell/src/dialogs/tm-about-dialog.ts`) has the §11.1 About tab in full. Its Reload
  goes through the error views' `TmErrorUpdates` (now exported as `ɵTmErrorUpdates` from
  `@tellma/core/errors`), with the busy, failed and Reload anyway states.
- **Shortcuts** (`shell/src/dialogs/tm-shortcuts-dialog.ts`) has four sections: General and This
  page from a snapshot of `TmShortcuts.active`, Lists and grids from `TM_GRID_KEYS` grouped by label
  key, and Launcher. Each is a table with `th scope=col` headers, filtered through
  `tmSearchNormalize`.
- **The launcher seam** (`shell/src/launcher/tm-launcher.ts`) has `state`, `query`, `show`,
  `close`, `setQuery` and `enterTenant`. It opens nothing, and the launcher checkpoint replaces its
  bodies.
- **`@tellma/core-ui/initials`** is a new entry point holding `tmInitials` and `tmAvatarTone`, moved
  from `/avatar` with git mv. `/avatar` re-exports them.
- **`tm-menu`** gains `panelClass`, a class on the menu's own panel. `TmMenuItem` gains
  `detailLabel`, which names the detail in the row's accessible name ("Acme, Switch tenant" while it
  shows "Switch").
- **Test support** (`tellma-core-testing`):
  - `TmTenantShellHarness`: what main shows, the error and its actions, the bar's names, tone,
    markers, slots, reserved boxes and history, and the marker popover and session menu, waiting
    for their chunk;
  - `TmSessionMenuHarness`: a `TmMenuHarness` plus the header, the tone and the tenant card;
  - `tmTestShellSlot()`.
- **Arabic** for the four new scopes, in `@tellma/locale-ar/shell-strings`.

### Deviations

1. **Report a problem and the About dialog's Diagnostics tab are omitted.** They need
   `TmSupport` and `/support`, which do not exist yet. About shows only its About tab.
2. **The session button is an eager button, not `button[tmMenuTriggerFor]`.** It mirrors the
   trigger's keys and ARIA (`aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, Enter, Space and
   the arrows), so the first frame carries no `@tellma/core-ui/menu`.
3. **The first frame draws the initials through the new `@tellma/core-ui/initials`.** The
   28 px `tm-avatar`, with the photo, arrives with the overlays chunk.
4. **The slot rows show in the session menu below md, not below sm.** §7.1 hides the slots
   below md, while §10.1 says below sm. Below md is the one that keeps every slot reachable.
5. **Mod+K is not registered.** The launcher seam opens nothing until the launcher checkpoint.
6. **The pinned bar's row stays at 0 px** until the pins land.
7. **About's Reload uses the error views' `TmErrorUpdates`**, now exported as `ɵTmErrorUpdates`
   from `@tellma/core/errors`, so About and the client-outdated view share one update path.
8. **A tenant switch creates a new tenant shell** (`TmRouteReuse` never reuses a route across
   tenants). The launcher closes and clears its query through `TmLauncher.enterTenant`.
9. **The shell has its own failed line with Try again** for a failure that is not a `TmApiError`,
   or when the error views cannot load.
10. **`TmMenu.panelClass` and `TmMenuItem.detailLabel` are new public surface** in
    `@tellma/core-ui/menu`.
11. **The Shortcuts dialog's Launcher section lists Shift+Enter beside Mod+Enter** for "Open the
    result in a new tab".

### Open risks

- **The first frame's `initialJs` is 0.27 KB under its 230 KB warning on Acme.** Any further
  eager addition to the primary, `/shell` or `/attention` will warn until savings are found.
- **The app bar's eager weight is the largest single addition to the first frame.** The CSS is the
  heaviest part of it. The alternative was a stylesheet per breakpoint in a lazy chunk, but that
  would paint the wrong bar until it loaded.
- **The overlays chunk is created in the bar's view container after the first render.** Its
  `tm-session-menu` input binding has to run before the first action. The host now runs
  `detectChanges()` right after creating it. Without that, a press made before the chunk lands threw
  NG0950 (a test caught it).
- **The router page-id fallback depends on `canceledNavigationResolution: 'computed'`**, which
  `provideTellma` sets. If that setting ever changes, Back stays disabled without the Navigation
  API.

5. The new public surfaces: `@tellma/core-ui/initials`, `TmMenu.panelClass`,

**R1-1 (blocking) — an existing route-host spec now fails deterministically.**
