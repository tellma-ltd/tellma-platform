# PC-048: List-page grid features: activation, driver, sort, select-all, row classes, gutter

## Implementer notes

Scope: spec 0010 §C.1 (row activation), §C.2 (two-way active row, `tmGridDriver`), §C.3 (sort
state and `aria-sort`), §C.5 (select-all across pages), §C.6 (`rowClass`, `rowHeaders`) and §C.7
(`scrollbar-gutter`), on `tm-grid` and, through the shared base and core, `tm-tree-grid`. Header
navigation and header actions (§C.4), `scrollToRow` (§C.8), tree `expandedIds` (§C.9), the
row-end column (§C.14) and popup editors (§C.15) are later checkpoints.

### What was built, and where (all under `client/projects/`)

- **Public surface** (`core/tellma-core-ui/grid/`):
  - `tm-grid-types.ts`: `TmGridRowActivation<T>` and `TmGridSort`.
  - `tm-grid-driver.ts`: `TmGridDriverTarget` and the `input[tmGridDriver]` directive. It sets
    `aria-controls` and `aria-activedescendant` and handles ArrowUp/Down, PageUp/Down, Enter and
    Mod+Enter. It skips IME composition, events that are already handled, and other modifiers.
  - `tm-grid-base.ts`:
    - new inputs `rowActivation`, `rowClass` and `rowHeaders`;
    - new models `activeRowId` and `orderBy`, and the output `rowActivate`;
    - `controlsId`, `activeDescendantId` and `pageStep`, plus `moveActiveRow()` and
      `activateRow()`;
    - dev errors for row activation on an editable grid and for a sortable column without a key.
  - `tm-grid-column.ts`: `sortable`.
- **Core** (`grid/internal/grid-core.ts`):
  - **Pointer activation:** press and release under 4 px, through a `click` option on the shared
    drag pipeline. A drag past the slop is the old range drag.
  - **Other gestures:** the middle button (press moves the active cell, `auxclick` opens), touch
    tap, and the 400 ms same-row guard.
  - **Keyboard:** Enter and Mod+Enter in row mode. F2 actuates the cell's link or button.
  - **Cell links:** a link keeps the browser's menu on right-click and long-press. The long-press
    observer in `menu/internal/long-press.ts` gained an `enabled` predicate for this.
  - **Active row:** `moveActiveRow`, and the two-way `activeRowId` sync effect (a consumer write
    moves and reveals the cell without focusing it; an unknown id is rewritten).
  - **Sort:** the header cycle, where a click sorts and a drag past the slop selects columns. A
    tap sorts. `headerSort` holds the per-header state, and the change is announced.
  - **Select-all:** adds or removes only the bound rows' ids, and uses the `checkedCountAcross`
    announcement when checked ids lie off the page.
  - **Rows and chrome:** the row view model's `activeRow`, `elementId` and `rowClass`. Without
    row headers, the chrome indices, the template tracks and the colcount shift.
- **View** (`grid/internal/grid-view.ts`, `grid-view.css`):
  - the scroller id, `aria-describedby` and the visually hidden hint;
  - the optional corner and row header, and the shifted checkbox-column indices;
  - the header `data-key`, `aria-sort` and the always-present 14 px sort slot (`--icon-size-sm`)
    with a hover preview;
  - row ids, classes and the active-row tint plus 2 px inline-start accent;
  - the `--tm-grid-row-bg/-ink/-weight` custom properties, and the `--muted` and `--emphasis`
    modifiers;
  - the `scrollbar-gutter: stable` scroller with its header and body background bands, and
    forced-colours rules.
- **Keys** (`grid/internal/grid-keymap.ts`):
  - `Mod+Enter` (`activateRow`, `when: 'rowActivation'`) joins the binding table, so it reaches
    `TM_GRID_KEYS`.
  - F2's condition became any-of (`['editable', 'rowActivation']`). A binding's `when` now
    accepts a list.
- **Token:** `--grid-active-row-bg` on `surface.selected`. The identity server's
  `wwwroot/css/tokens.css` was refreshed.
- **Strings:** English and Arabic for:
  - the row-activation hint;
  - the `sorted`, `sortCleared` and `checkedCountAcross` announcements;
  - the new `openRowNewTab` key label, and the reworded Enter and F2 labels.
- **Harness** (`core/tellma-core-ui-testing/grid-harness.ts`): `activateRow(i)`,
  `getActiveRowIndex()`, `sortBy(key, direction?)`, `getSort()` and the `TmGridHarnessSort` type.
- **Docs:** a `SearchPage` example in `tm-grid.examples.ts`, with the docs-examples harness taught
  the driver and the `rowActivation`/`sortable` markers.
- **Showcase:** the `grid-search-page` story (`internal/showcase/src/app/grid/`):
  - a driver search field;
  - a paged source of 230 rows in pages of 50, ordered by the two-way sort;
  - a real link in the Name cell;
  - muted inactive rows and an optional emphasized totals row;
  - no row headers;
  - a consumer "jump" write;
  - readouts for the Playwright battery.

### Deviations (obvious corrections, also in the commit message)

1. **`Mod+Enter` in `TM_GRID_KEYS`.**
   - §C.1 and §C.12 require it, but it collides with spec 0027 §4.3: the page family lists
     `Mod+Enter`, and the registry refuses every `TM_GRID_KEYS` entry with a modifier.
   - This is flagged for the orchestrator, with a WATCHLIST line under 0027.
2. **F2 under row activation reuses the `edit` intent.** The core routes it to the cell's
   link or button. The binding's `when` became an any-of list, so the published key list keeps
   one F2 row.
3. **Sort announcements fire only on grid gestures.** A consumer write to `orderBy`, such as a
   restored state, shows silently. §C.3 says "a change announces", which read literally would
   speak on every page load that restores a sort.
4. **The 400 ms guard swallows an activation and still returns `true`.** This way a driver keeps
   consuming the Enter. `false` stays reserved for "no such row", per §C.2.
5. **Moving with no active row starts at the first row.** A numeric `moveActiveRow` does this in
   either direction. §C.2 specifies only ArrowDown.
6. **A `null` write to `activeRowId` is treated like an unknown id.** While rows exist and a
   cell is active, it is rewritten with the actual row.
7. **Mod+click in row mode also adds a range.** It still activates with `newTab: true`, so both
   §C.1 table rows hold.
8. **Column headers carry `data-key`.** The harness's `sortBy(key)` and `getSort()` find the
   column by key.
9. **The sort slot uses the existing `--icon-size-sm` (14 px).** No new token was added.
10. **The gutter's body band is transparent.** The grid body has no background of its own, since
    the cells are transparent, so a transparent band is the body background. The header band
    uses the header colour plus its bottom rule.
11. **The active-row accent is a `::before` on the row's first painted cell.** That is the
    checkbox cell on a selectable grid.
12. **Long-press now takes an `enabled` predicate.** `menu/internal/long-press.ts`
    (`ɵ`-internal) gains it so that a press on a cell link arms no timer.
13. **The `./grid` and primary ceilings rise.** See Budgets.
14. **The "last column clears the scrollbar gutter" e2e moved to `grid-gutter.spec.ts`.**
    - It left `grid-date.spec.ts`, so it now runs with classic scrollbars shown, in both
      directions.
    - With hidden scrollbars (headless Chromium's default), `scrollbar-gutter: stable` still
      reserves the strip but caps the scroll range as if it were not there. The last column
      therefore can never clear it, and the old test failed deterministically: off by 15 px.
    - With scrollbars shown, the cell clears the client box in LTR and RTL.

### Budgets

- **`./grid`, 39 → 42 KB.** Measured 38.73 → 41.92 KB (+3.19 KB), with a budgetNotes entry.
  - About 0.8 KB is template and stylesheet text, which the build ships twice in the decorator

  - Savings taken: −0.10 KB. One cell test is shared by the press, middle-click and tap paths,

- **`@tellma/core-ui` (primary), 8.5 → 9 KB.** Measured 8.48 → 8.61 KB (+0.13 KB), all of it the
  new English strings. §C.11 does not list the primary.

### Open risks, and what reviewers should examine hardest

- **The click-versus-drag split in `beginDrag`.** This is the shared pointer pipeline. Moves
  under the slop are ignored until a drag starts, and a release without a drag calls `onClick`.
  Check that range drags, column drags and the existing mouse e2e are unchanged. The old e2e
  battery was rerun.
- **The `activeRowId` sync effect.** It is created after the reconcile effect, holds a `synced`
  variable, and a consumer write wins. Look at write loops, and at its interplay with data swaps
  and tree expansion (`expandAncestorsOf`).
- **The press-frame probe e2e.** It is PR-tier, not nightly. A rAF scheduled from a document
  pointerdown listener runs after Angular's scheduler rAF, which the grid's own handler
  registered first, so the check is deterministic rather than timing-based.
- **The showcase shell is wider than a phone (`.shell-controls` is 483 px).** In RTL the page
  therefore scrolls sideways, and cells at the inline start sit outside the visual viewport. The
  touch spec targets the Qty column for this reason. The shell itself is untouched.
- **The `.tm-grid__cell` base now sets `background-color`, `color` and `font-weight` from the
  row custom properties.** When unset, they resolve to transparent and inherit. Check readonly
  column tints, the placeholder rule and the selection layers for specificity regressions.
- **The hidden-scrollbar gutter quirk (deviation 14) has wider reach.** Any environment that
  hides classic scrollbars while keeping a stable gutter would leave the last column partly under
  an empty strip. Headless runs are such an environment, and so would be a page that hides
  scrollbars through CSS. The grid's reveal math is unchanged.
- **Keyboard-driven sort is not covered here.** It needs §C.4's header navigation, so the DoD's
  "keyboard-only e2e for sort" lands with that checkpoint.
