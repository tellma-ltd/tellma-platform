# PC-163: Pinned bar, TmPins and the Manage pins dialog

## Implementer notes

### What was built

Spec 0027 §8 (all but the model, which PC-107 shipped in the primary) and §5.3's pin-removal
focus rule, in `@tellma/core/shell` (`client/projects/core/tellma-core/shell/src/pins/`).

- **`TmPins`** — `tm-pins.ts`, root, exported from `./shell` with `TmPinView` and
  `TmPinFolderView`.
  - `items`, `current`, `canPinCurrent`, `visible`. The views keep their identity across writes
    (a pin's `label`, `state` and `current` are its own signals), so the bar re-renders only
    what changed.
  - `pinCurrent`, `update`, `move`, `remove`, `setVisible`, `addFolder`, `removeFolder`,
    `replace`. Each writes through `TmPreferences.set` and reports
    `tm.shell.pins-changed { op, via, count }`, with `count` bucketed `0`, `1-5`, `6-15`, `16+`;
    `setVisible` reports `tm.shell.pinned-bar-toggled { visible }`. `tmPinsVia` sets the `via`
    for an edit made by the bar's menus, the popover, a drag or the dialog.
  - Internal members the shell's chunks read: `ɵrefusal` (unpinnable, too long, full),
    `ɵfrozen` (spec 0029 §7's `tm.entity.tenantFrozen` reason) and `ɵlauncherCommands`.
  - The edits are pure functions in `tm-pin-rules.ts` (`tmPinsAppend`, `tmPinsUpdate`,
    `tmPinsMove`, `tmPinsRemove`, `tmPinsAddFolder`, `tmPinsRemoveFolder`,
    `tmPinsRenameFolder`, `tmPinFolderNameProblem`), beside the existing view, capture, label
    and current-pin rules. They enforce the caps, one level and the folder-name rule, and refuse
    bad input with a `RangeError`.
- **`TmPinnedBar`** — `tm-pinned-bar.ts`, internal (the tenant shell renders it in the frame's
  third row while `TmPins.visible()`).
  - Chips with the current-page styling and `aria-current="page"`; folder chips (menu buttons)
    after a 1 × 16 separator; More; the pin control (`Alt+Shift+P`, `aria-keyshortcuts`,
    filled when pinned, disabled with its reason at 30 pins, past 512 characters and while
    Frozen, where the shortcut does nothing); Manage pins (`settings-2`) in every state; the
    empty lines (short below sm); pills below md, the current one first.
  - The fit (§8.7): a hidden, out-of-flow, `aria-hidden` measuring row renders every item as
    the bar shows it, at the current chip's weight, with locks. Its widths are cached per item
    and re-read in one `afterRenderEffect` read phase whenever the items, a label, a state, the
    breakpoint, the language, the direction or the loaded faces change. A `ResizeObserver`
    refits from the cached widths and renders in the same callback. Each shown chip takes its
    measured width as its box, so becoming current, hover, focus and (≥md) navigation move
    nothing. The list is `visibility: hidden` until the first fit, which waits for
    `document.fonts` (`status === 'loaded'`, else `ready`); `loadingdone` re-reads. The fonts
    seam is the internal `TM_PINNED_BAR_FONTS` token, so tests drive a late face.
  - One tab stop (current, else first) with direction-mapped arrows, Home and End; Enter, Space
    and Down open a menu chip; a plain click is a router navigation.
  - Revoked and unavailable chips: dimmed, a lock, focusable, never links; their menu says why
    and offers Unpin.
  - Preload: after the first render, `TmUrls.preload(href)` once per `ok` pin.
  - Cold launch (§7.5's pinned-bar column): three 64 px chip skeletons while the sync is pending,
    an empty row once it failed (the tenant shell passes its failure in).
- **`TmPinsUi`** — `tm-pins-ui.ts`, provided by the tenant shell: the pin control's state and
  reason, the press (control, shortcut, launcher), the reveal after a pin (popover when the new
  chip shows, else the toast "Pinned {label}." once), the announcements, the launcher's
  Pinned-band commands and the overlays' loader (idle, or the bar's first hover or focus; a
  press that beats it acts when it lands).
- **The overlays chunk** — `tm-pin-overlays.ts` (`tm-pin-overlays`, 4.73 KB against 5): one
  `tm-menu` re-filled per opener (More with links, folder submenus and locked rows, the current
  pin's item with `aria-current`; a folder's pins or "No pages in this folder"; a revoked pin's
  message and Unpin), the pin popover ("Pinned" or "Edit pin": name, folder select with "New
  folder…" and its field, Remove and Done; Enter or Done applies, Escape keeps, focus returns to
  the control), and drag and drop along the bar (`tm.pin`, a 2 px insertion line, a 500 ms hover
  that opens a folder chip's menu so a pin drops inside, announcements).
- **The Manage pins dialog** — `tm-manage-pins-dialog.ts` (`tm-manage-pins-dialog`, 5.74 KB
  against 6, within spec 0027's 6 KB for the dialogs), opened through the session panels'
  `TM_SHELL_DIALOGS_LOAD` beside About and Shortcuts, so it opens at once in a placeholder of
  its final height and returns focus to the opener. A `tm-reorder-list` of the top-level items,
  each folder row with its own nested list accepting `tm.pin`; per row an icon (the entry's,
  else the route kind's), the label (`dir="auto"`), Rename (inline field), Move to (menu) and
  Remove (focus to the next row's handle, else New folder; §5.3); revoked rows with a lock and
  "No access". Footer: New folder, Cancel, Save, under the "Show the pinned bar" switch, which
  applies at once. It edits a copy; Save calls `TmPins.replace`, Cancel and Escape discard;
  Save is disabled with the Frozen reason.
- **Launcher (D-337, D-346)** — `tm-launcher-commands.ts` overrides `TM_LAUNCHER_COMMANDS`'s root
  factory from inside `./shell` (no new export) with `inject(TmPins).ɵlauncherCommands`: "Pin
  this page" or "Edit pin", and "Manage pins…". `settings-2` joins the launcher's lazily
  registered icons.
- **Icons (D-248)** — the bar, the overlays and the dialog's buttons draw their glyphs from
  per-icon lucide imports through `[svg]` or menu icon data, so they register no names. The
  dialog's row icons are entry and kind names, registered by the existing lazy
  `tm-launcher-icons` chunk, which registers only the names the app lacks.
- **Component library** — `TmMenuLink.current` (the row renders `aria-current="page"`) and
  `TmReorderList.itemKind` (rows drag under their own kind, so a list and its folders' lists
  exchange rows; `received` reports a row from another list).
- **Strings** — English `tm.pins` (eager), `tm.pinOverlays` and `tm.managePins` (each registered
  by its chunk). Arabic in a new `@tellma/locale-ar/pins-strings` (1.13 KB against 1.5), which
  `tmArabic()` loads as its seventh part: `./shell-strings` is at its ceiling (2.50 of 2.5).
  Path mappings added to `client/tsconfig.json` and the Acme client's `tsconfig.json`.
- **Testing** — `TmPinnedBarHarness` and `TmPinnedBarChipHarness` in `@tellma/core-testing`, and
  `TmTenantShellHarness.getPinnedBar()`.
- **Docs** — `@tellma/core`'s README (the `/shell` row, a pinned bar paragraph with a `TmPins`
  example) and `@tellma/locale-ar`'s README (the pins part, seven entry points).

### Deviations

1. **`./shell` is 19.48 KB against spec 0027's 16 KB for the eager code** (10.92 before,
   +8.56), and with it **initialJs is over the 230 warn level: Acme 234.07 KB (224.70 before) and
   the fixture app 233.16 KB**, so
   `lint:test`'s fixture build test (`exits 0 with no problem` and the four output checks that
   expect no other problem) fails on the budget warning. The ceiling goes from 11 to 19.5 (within
   the addition rounded up to the next 0.5) and records the floor, as D-337 did for the launcher
   panel; neither the spec budget nor the 230 line is raised. The itemization and the moves not
   taken are in the `./shell` budget note. Everything left is first-frame or a synchronous answer
   the spec requires (§1's whole chrome on a warm launch with no request, §8.7's fit in the first
   tick, §8.4's synchronous edits, §8.5's and §14's control filling in the click's frame). Moves
   that would bend the spec, with rough savings: TmPins' edits other than `pinCurrent` loading at
   idle with the overlays (about 1.4 KB; a public call before idle would act late); the bar's
   keys, the reveal and the toast in the overlays chunk (about 0.7 KB; a key or a pin that beats
   the chunk would act late); the whole bar after the first paint (about 7 KB; breaks §1 and
   §14's `shell-warm.spec`). Only the last one reaches both 16 and 230. This is also `./shell`'s
   third raise since D-291's re-split; the itemization is offered as that re-split.
2. A folder chip that holds the current pin takes the current styling (the spec only says it of
   a pin's chip and of More), so the routed page is always marked in the bar.
3. Drops onto More are ignored; More is not a drop target (the spec does not say).
4. `TmMenuLink.current` and `TmReorderList.itemKind` are new component-library members, needed
   for More's `aria-current` and the dialog's nested lists (spec 0023 §8.4 is silent on rows
   moving between lists).
5. `tmPinsRenameFolder`: the dialog renames folders as well as pins (§8.10's Rename on each
   row); `TmPins` has no folder rename, so the dialog edits its copy and Save replaces.
6. The bar drags through `TmDragDrop`'s internal begin, delegated on the bar, rather than one
   `tmDragSource` directive per chip: the chips re-render on every fit, and one delegated
   listener keeps the drag code in the overlays chunk.
7. The dialog is its own deferred chunk under the session panels' dialog loader, like About and
   Shortcuts, rather than a shared "dialogs chunk".
8. Strings the spec does not word: the Shortcuts row ("Pin or unpin this page"), the popover's
   blank and too-long name problems, the dialog's default folder names ("New folder",
   "New folder 2", …) and its ten-folder reason, and the revoked chips' accessible names.
9. Popover Remove returns focus to the pin control (§8.5 says so for Done and Escape).
10. Below md the current pill keeps the pill radius and drops the chip's block-end bar, which a
    teal tint replaces (§8.1).
11. `TmPinnedBar` is not exported: only the tenant shell renders it, and the harness reaches it.

### Open risks

- The pinned row is open from a cold launch's first frame unless the stored preference hides
  it. On a new device whose prefs are not yet synced, a user who hid the bar sees one 32 px
  shift when the preferences land.
- The fit relies on `document.fonts`; a browser that never resolves `ready` keeps the chips
  hidden (the row still holds its height and the controls work).
- Drag and drop is tested with synthetic events in Chromium only.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- The fit in `tm-pinned-bar.ts`: the read phase's tracking (no feedback loop between the fitted
  count and the widths), the ResizeObserver's synchronous refit and render, the fonts wait, and
  whether any state change can move a chip on hover, focus or ≥md navigation.
- The `./shell` itemization in the budget note: whether any of it can leave the first frame
  without breaking the spec.
- The edit rules' refusals and caps against §8.2, and `replace`'s handling of a stale copy.
- The dialog's focus after Remove, Move to and New folder, and the launcher's focus after a
  dialog it opened closes.

*The implementer's options, against the spec:*
- **(a) TmPins' edits other than `pinCurrent` at idle**: measured **0.95** (methods 0.16, rules
  0.72, their messages), not 1.4. It bends §8.4. `update`, `move`, `addFolder`, `removeFolder` and
  `replace` are public synchronous methods whose writes change "the bar … in the same frame".
  `addFolder` returns the new id and throws on a bad name synchronously. A deferred body would make
  an app's call before idle act late, or throw late. `remove` must stay in any case, because
  `Alt+Shift+P` "unpins at once" (§8.5).
- **(b) the bar's keys, the reveal and the toast into the overlays chunk**: measured **0.52**
  (keys 0.21, press, reveal and toast 0.32). The two halves differ:
  - The press, reveal and toast cannot move. §8.5 step 2 says "the control fills in the click's
    frame", and §14 says "a new pin … appear[s] in the click's frame". The toast is the only
    feedback when the chip goes into More, and TmToasts is already eager.
  - The roving keys (0.21) can move with replay, the way D-346 handles a pending launcher open,
    because §8.6 sets no frame for focus moves. The bar's `focusin` already starts the chunk's load.
    I count this as a small bend: an arrow pressed before idle would act late.
- **(c) the bar after the first paint**: ≈7. It breaks §14 ("Instant warm launch. The chrome
  renders from the store, the cookie and the bundle", pinned by `shell-warm.spec`), §7.5 (warm
  pinned bar = "the pins") and §8.7 (the first fit "in the tick that first renders the bar").
  Modulepreloading the chunk would keep it in initialJs, so (c) can only reach 230 by rendering the
  bar late.

**R1-2 (major): removing the last chip loses focus, and leaves a stale refocus that later
pulls focus onto a chip.**
- Where: `tm-pinned-bar.ts:605` (the read phase returns early when `measureEl()` is undefined),
  `:651-659` (`port.unpin` sets `#refocus`) and `:778-795` (`#settled` is the only consumer).
- Failure: the bar has one pin, the user is on its page with focus on its chip, and presses
  `Alt+Shift+P`. `tm-pins-ui.ts:318-323` passes `onChip = true`, so `#refocus = 0`. Its menu's
  Unpin on a lone revoked chip does the same (`tm-pin-overlays.ts:424`). `rows()` becomes empty,
  so the `@else` branch with the list and the measuring row is destroyed and `measureEl()` is
  undefined. The read phase returns before `#settled`. The chip that had focus is gone, so focus
  falls to `body`, against §5.3 ("else the pin control").
- `#refocus` also stays 0, so the next render that brings a chip back runs it from `#settled`.
  Take a user typing in a page field while another tab pins a page: the pins follow across tabs
  (§8.4, spec 0025 §9.3), this tab's bar renders the chip, and focus jumps from the field to the
  chip. A folder saved from Manage pins into the empty bar can pull focus off the Manage pins
  button the dialog returned it to. A pin made from this tab's own control hides the bug, because
  the popover's own afterRenderEffect focuses its name field after the stale refocus.
- Fix: resolve the refocus at the unpin itself whenever the removal empties the bar (no pins
  remain, so focus the control after the render), or run the refocus branch of `#settled` before
  the `measureEl()` gate, falling back to the control. Clear `#refocus` whenever it is consumed.
  Add harness tests: Alt+Shift+P on the only chip focuses the pin control; a pin added
  afterwards through `TmPreferences` (another tab's write) leaves focus where it was.

**R1-3 (minor): below `md`, a drag drops at the stored index while the pills show the current
one first, so a drop beside the leading pill lands elsewhere.**
- Where: `tm-pin-overlays.ts:563-571` computes `index` from `rest`, which is `TmPins.items()`
  order, while the bar renders `#ordered` (`tm-pinned-bar.ts:541-546`, current first below md).
- Failure: at 600 px with pins A, B, C on C's page, the pills show C, A, B. The user drags B onto
  the inline-end half of C, aiming for C, B, A. `at` is C's stored index, 1, so `index` is 2: the
  stored order stays A, C, B and the pills still show C, A, B. The line drew between C and A, the
  drop did not land there, and "position 3 of 3" is announced.
- Fix: compute the insertion from the displayed order. Map the neighbour the line sits beside back
  to its stored position, and treat "after the lead pill" as "before the first non-current item in
  stored order". Add a below-md drag test.

**R1-4 (minor): spec rules without a test that would fail if they broke.**
- `tm-pinned-bar.spec.ts:498` is titled "…which renames and files it", but it only renames. The
  popover's folder select, and New folder… with its name field, its duplicate refusal and its
  `addFolder`-then-`update` path (`tm-pin-overlays.ts:374-385`), are untested (§8.5, §8.8).
- Escape or an outside click on the popover keeps the pin, with focus back on the control (§8.5
  step 4): untested.
- `Alt+Shift+P` doing nothing while Frozen (§8.1): only the control's disabled state is asserted
  (`:566`).
- The dialog's `received` path, a pin dragged from the top list into a folder's list and back
  (`tm-manage-pins-dialog.ts:531-536`, §8.10 "accepts tm.pin"): no dialog test drags between
  lists. Escape discarding the copy, and Save overwriting a change made meanwhile (§8.10), are not
  asserted either.
- The Manage pins button's focus return after the dialog closes (§8.10 "Focus returns to the
  opener"): `:630-637` closes the dialog but does not check focus.

Fix: add these cases; R1-2's two tests cover §5.3's last branch.
