# PC-030: Form controls

## Implementer notes

### What was built, and where

All paths are under `client/` unless stated. Six new entry points in
`projects/core/tellma-core-ui/`, each with `ng-package.json`, `public-api.ts`, unit and harness
specs, a budget, an API golden (`api/core-ui.<ep>.api.md`), co-located `*.examples.ts` files
(compiled by `docs-examples.spec.ts`, `@tmGroup form-control`) and a showcase story with the
entry point's id. Harnesses are in `projects/core/tellma-core-ui-testing/` (README updated).

- **`radio`** (spec 0023 §6.1). `TmRadioGroup<T>` (`tm-radio-group`, `FormValueControl<T | null>`)
  and the definition component `TmRadio<T>` (`tm-radio`, empty template). A `role="radiogroup"`
  of native radios sharing one generated `name`, with a roving `tabIndex` (checked radio, else the
  first enabled one). The arrows are handled by the group through
  `ɵtmRadioArrowTarget` (new, `private/tm-radio-keys.ts`): Up/Left(mapped) previous,
  Down/Right(mapped) next, wrapping over enabled radios, direction read from
  `getComputedStyle(host).direction` at keydown. Readonly reverts the native check. Named by
  `ariaLabel`, else by the enclosing `tm-form-field`'s label (`setLabelId`). A radio's hint is
  its `aria-describedby`. Rows at `--field-option-height` per size; `orientation`.
- **`segmented`** (§6.1). `TmSegmented<T>` and `TmSegment<T>` (value, label, icon registry name,
  disabled). Same native-radio and arrow model as radio, in a sunken track at the field height;
  the checked segment is a card with a hairline and `shadow-xs`. `labels`: `always` | `never` |
  a `TmBreakpoint`, implemented with `@container (width < N)` rules against the nearest
  inline-size query container; a `ResizeObserver` (started after first render) reads whether the
  first collapsible label is hidden and turns the icon-only tooltip on (`ɵTmSegmentTooltip`,
  driving the existing `ɵTmTooltipController`). Icon segments keep `aria-label` = label; their
  label span is `aria-hidden`.
- **`switch`** (§6.1). `TmSwitch` (`FormCheckboxControl`): a visually hidden native checkbox with
  `role="switch"` stretched over the padded track, `checked` model, `labelPosition`. The knob's
  `inset-inline-start` transition is the only motion; static under reduced motion. Readonly
  reverts the native change and sets `aria-readonly`.
- **`search-field`** (§6.2). `TmSearch` (`input[tmSearch]`, exportAs `tmSearch`): `value` per
  input event; `search` on the leading edge (first input after a quiet `debounce`) and trailing
  edge; a held key (`repeat`) waits for `keyup` (or blur); IME waits for `compositionend`
  (keyCode 229 ignored); Enter and clear emit at once; never the last emitted value; `flush()`.
  `clear()` and Escape select the text and run `document.execCommand('delete')` (fallback: set
  the value and dispatch `input`), so Mod+Z restores it. Escape on non-empty text stops
  propagation; on empty text it propagates. The native `search` event is stopped at the input
  (capture listener) so it cannot be confused with the `search` output. `TmSearchField`: a
  `tm-form-field` (ViewEncapsulation.None, namespaced selectors) with a `tmPrefix` magnifier, the
  input, and a `tmSuffix` clear button whose slot is always reserved (hidden, `aria-hidden`,
  `tabindex=-1` while empty).
- **`date-range`** (§6.3). `TmDateRange` (`FormValueControl<TmDateRangeValue>`) and
  `tmDateRangeOrder(path)` (`validate`, kind `dateRangeOrder`). Two `tm-form-field` +
  `tm-date-picker` pairs in a labelled `role="group"`; from.maxDate = earlier(maxDate, to),
  to.minDate = later(minDate, from). The order error and the "to" end's own parse error show on
  "to"; everything else on "from".
- **`date-time`** (§6.4). `TmTimeField` (`FormValueControl<string | null>`, `'HH:mm'`):
  formatting through `tmFormatTime` over a fixed UTC instant; parsing in
  `internal/tm-time-of-day.ts` (any `\p{Nd}` digits, `14`, `1430`, `14:30`, the locale's
  day-period words read from Intl's h12 parts plus Latin am/pm, locale literal words such as
  fr-CA "h"); reformat on blur (when edited) and Enter; Up/Down by `step`, wrapping at midnight,
  starting from the clock floored to the step when empty. `TmDateTimeField` (`value` an instant):
  a date picker, a time field and the always-shown zone label (`tmFormatZoneOffset` at the value,
  else now) at the group's inline end. Wall time ↔ instant in `internal/tm-zone-wall-time.ts`:
  a gap time becomes the transition instant (binary search on the minute the offset changes); a
  repeated time takes the earlier instant; writes are `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ss±HH:mm` (`+00:00` for
  UTC, no fraction).
- **Composite parse errors.** Both composites use their own `transformedValue` over the parts.
  A child's parse errors (read from the child's public `parseErrors`) are tagged with `end` or
  `part` and returned as the composite's own `parse` errors, so a bound form sees them; an
  `effect` re-reads the parts when a child's parse errors change (a child raises them around its
  own write, after the value change has already been read). The bound echo of the composite's own
  kinds is filtered from `errors` before routing, so nothing shows twice.
- **Strings** (§10): `searchField.*`, `dateRange.*`, `dateTime.*`, `errors.dateRangeOrder`,
  `errors.dateTimeIncomplete`, plus `errors.parseTime` (see deviations); Arabic twins in
  `projects/locale/tellma-locale-ar/strings-ar.ts`.
- **Tokens** (§9): `radio`, `segmented`, `switch`, `dateTime` groups in
  `tellma-core-ui-tokens/presets/tellma-default.ts`; `tokens.spec.ts` pins the emitted geometry.
  Identity's copied `tokens.css` refreshed (`src/apps/Tellma.Identity/wwwroot/css/tokens.css`).
- **Global sheet** `styles/tm-search.css` (`.tm-search`: native cancel and decoration hidden),
  registered in package.json `tellma.docs.globalStyles`, angular.json showcase `styles` and the
  README, as the other bare directives' sheets are.
- **Showcase**: stories `radio`, `segmented`, `switch`, `search-field`, `date-range`,
  `date-time` (every state named by §6; both themes and directions via the query params); icons
  `table-2`, `layout-grid`, `list` registered.
- **E2E** (`e2e/specs/`): `radio`, `segmented`, `switch`, `search-field`, `date-range`,
  `date-time` (axe floor in light/dark × LTR/RTL each; RTL arrows and a live direction switch for
  radio and segmented; clear and Escape undone by Mod+Z tagged `@cross-engine`; the zone label
  across Beirut's DST change in LTR and RTL; forced colors for the active segment and the switch
  on; the knob under reduced motion) and `form-controls-touch.spec.ts` (Pixel 7 project,
  segments and switch rows at least 24 px; added to the chromium `testIgnore` and the touch
  `testMatch`).
- `ARCHITECTURE.md`: one sentence that the date-time field always shows the zone label.

### Deviations

1. **`TmDatePicker.parseErrors` is public** (a new readonly signal on an existing component). The
   composites must see a child's unreadable text to report it as their own `parse` error, and the
   picker's `transformedValue` parse errors are otherwise private. `TmTimeField` exposes the same
   member.
2. **The no-write rule compares at the minute the parts show**, not the exact instant. §6.4 says
   "compared as instants"; read literally, re-reading `11:00` from a bound `11:00:30` would write
   `11:00:00`. Comparing the bound value floored to the minute with the read instant keeps the
   rule's intent (a re-read writes nothing) for every bound value, fraction and seconds included.
3. **`errors.parseTime`** "Enter a time like {example}" (Arabic twin added): §10 lists no
   message for the time field's `parse` error; the date picker's is date-specific.
4. **`styles/tm-search.css`**, a global sheet for the bare directive (hiding the native search
   cancel button needs a non-encapsulated rule on the consumer's input).
5. **Segmented `labels` breakpoints are container queries against the nearest inline-size
   query container**; with no container the labels show. §6.1 says "while the container is at
   least this wide" without naming which container; the host cannot be its own container because
   its width is its content's.
6. **`tm-date-range` `layout` defaults to `stacked`** (§6.3 gives no default; stacked is the
   filter-popover case it names first).
7. **en-GB writes `0:05` for times before 10:00** (`tmFormatTime` uses `hour: 'numeric'`), so
   the spec's "14:30" example holds but the morning hours carry no leading zero. Left as the
   formatter does it rather than special-casing the field.
8. **An incomplete pair writes `null`** with `dateTimeIncomplete` (the spec names the error but
   not the value); clearing one part of a bound instant therefore writes `null`.
9. **`TmTimeField` has an internal `ɵclockZone` input** so the date-time field's time part starts
   stepping from the clock in the field's `timeZone`, not the ambient one.

### Colour choice

The radio hint, the segment text and the zone label use `--text-secondary`, not
`--text-muted`: axe flagged `--text-muted` below 4.5:1 against the field and track surfaces, and
these are readable text, not placeholders.

### Open risks

- The composite parse-error plumbing depends on Signal Forms internals as observed in 22.0.5:
  a child's parse errors reset when its model changes and are re-raised after its write, which
  is why the composites re-read in an effect. A framework change in that ordering would show as
  a missing or stale `parse` error (harness specs pin both composites).
- Segmented's icon-only switch reads the computed display of a label inside a
  `ResizeObserver`; a consumer with no query container always sees labels.
- `execCommand('delete')` is deprecated but still the only undoable programmatic edit in all
  three engines; the cross-engine e2e pins it.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- `date-time/tm-date-time-field.ts` `readParts`, `syncParts` and the two effects: gap
  read-back (the parts reformat to the transition time), zone change (re-sync without a write),
  and the no-write comparison.
- `date-time/internal/tm-zone-wall-time.ts` (gap/overlap search) and
  `date-time/internal/tm-time-of-day.ts` (parser acceptance and refusals across scripts).
- `search-field/tm-search.ts` edge logic (leading/trailing, held key, IME, Enter, equal values).
- Error routing in `date-range/tm-date-range.ts` (which end shows what; the bound `parse`
  echo filtered).
