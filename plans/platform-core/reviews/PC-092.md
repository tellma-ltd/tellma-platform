# PC-092: AcmeFeature: entities, actions, settings and development data

## Implementer notes

### What was built, and where

- **Acme's feature (`distributions/acme/src/Tellma.Distro.Acme`).** The empty `AcmeFeature` that
  the Acme client app's checkpoint created is extended here, as its review asked, not replaced.
  - `AcmeFeature` is `[Requires(gl)]`. It contributes the entities, services, settings, strings,
    the center tree that replaces GL's default, and the sample-data step.
  - `AcmeComposition` now composes GL with `MyCenter` as the center leaf.
  - `Entities/`:
    - `Currency` is `[Cacheable]`.
    - `Customer` is activatable, with a `CustomerType` enum, a decimal `CreditLimit`, an `ImageId`
      of blob kind `customer-image` shown as an avatar, and a `Contacts` child.
    - `SalesInvoice` has `[DisplayNameSource]`, a `Lines` child whose `CenterId` points at
      `gl.Center`, and an `Attachments` child of kind `invoice-attachment`.
    - `MyCenter` adds `ManagerId`, pointing at `core.User`.
  - `Services/`:
    - `CustomerService` has `set-credit-limit` (`SetCreditLimitArguments`), and refuses a negative
      limit both on a save and on the action.
    - `SalesInvoiceService` has `close`.
    - `RelatedDocumentsContributor` is the invoices' `related-documents` details extra.
  - `AcmeSettings` holds the five keys. `acme.integration.marminApiKey` is a secret.
    `AcmeValidationCodes` holds the feature's error codes.
  - `Resources/Strings.resx` and `Strings.ar.resx` hold the labels of every entity, member,
    action, enum value and setting, plus the validation messages (64 keys each). The library's
    neutral language is `en`.
- **Development data (`Development/`).**
  - `AcmeSampleCenters` is the 40-node, four-level tree. It also lists the four centers to
    deactivate and the active sales centers that get invoiced.
  - `AcmeSampleData` is the deterministic data:
    - two currencies;
    - 60 customers of all three types, in both currencies, 10 with a generated portrait
      (`SamplePortraits`), some with contacts, four deactivated;
    - 20 invoices, the six oldest closed.
  - `AcmeDevelopmentPrincipals` are the end-to-end principals.
  - `AcmeSampleDataStep` is `acme.sample-data`, version 1, Order 200. It runs only in
    Development, as the system, through the stacks, inserting only the rows that are missing. In
    order it writes:
    1. currencies;
    2. customers (portraits staged first), then deactivates the four;
    3. the roles `Sales` and `CenterReaders` (gl.Center Read filtered to `CenterType = 'Sale'`);
    4. the principals;
    5. invoices, then closes the six;
    6. deactivates the four centers.
- **The e2e-principal invite issue, resolved by binding subjects in Development.** Provisioning
  runs in the migrator, where no identity server is reachable, so an invitation cannot be sent.
  Instead:
  - Core gained the `IDevelopmentSubjects` seam (Abstractions). `DevelopmentSubjects` and
    `DevelopmentSubjectsEffect<TUser>` record each user a held save creates with its fixed
    subject, as an invitation would.
    - The seam works only in Development and only in the system scope.
    - `TenantBootstrapper` binds the development administrator through the same seam; the old
      `BootstrapSubject` was renamed.
    - `CoreFeature` registers the effect.
  - Identity gained `TellmaIdentity:Seed:DevUsers`. The in-proc identity server seeds each user
    with a fixed subject (`IdentitySeeder.EnsureDevUsersAsync`), validated by `ValidateDevUsers`
    and audited as `DevUserSeeded`.
  - `appsettings.Development.json` seeds `sales@localhost`, `centers@localhost` and
    `sandbox@localhost`, with the subjects `AcmeDevelopmentPrincipals` binds.
  - The step records the catalog membership hints for the individuals it creates.
  - The service account "Acme Integration" is created without credentials.
  - `sandbox@localhost` exists only in tenant 2.
- **Configuration.** `appsettings.Development.json` turns `Tellma:Support:Enabled` on. Identity
  stays InProc, and there is no `Tellma:Blobs:Azure`.
- **Migration.** Acme's `InitialTenant` was regenerated with dotnet-ef (`20261121233156`). It now
  holds:
  - the `acme` tables;
  - `gl.Centers.ManagerId`, with its foreign key and index;
  - the customers' image foreign key.
- **Docs.**
  - The library README, the distribution README and the test READMEs.
  - ARCHITECTURE.md: the Acme layout line, and the migrator paragraph on how development
    principals are bound with no invitation.

### Deviations

1. **Development principals are bound, not invited.**
   - The spec invites the e2e principals, but the migrator cannot reach an identity server.
   - So each principal gets a fixed subject that the in-proc identity server seeds, through the
     new `IDevelopmentSubjects` seam and `Seed:DevUsers`.
   - The step records the membership hints itself.
   - This needs Ahmad's ruling.
2. **The resource audit runs in Web.Tests, not IntegrationTests.** It needs the composed model
   but no database.
3. **`related-documents` takes several targets.**
   - It excludes the targets themselves.
   - It is omitted beyond 128 targets, the size of one key list.
4. **The invoice-number padding is validated to 1–12.** The spec gives no range.
5. **`Acme_Settings_Numbering` and `Acme_Settings_NextNumber` ship ahead of the settings tab.**
   They are listed as pending in the audit.
6. **Sample rows are inserted only when missing**, so a version bump re-runs the step without
   duplicating anything.
7. **Customer pictures are generated PNGs** (`SamplePortraits`), not files checked into the repo.
8. **`Gl_Center_ManagerId` ships in Acme's strings**, because Acme adds the member.
9. **The none-outside-Development test runs a real Production `migrate` and `provision`.** It
   uses an ACS email endpoint that nothing sends to.

### Open risks

- **The principals' subjects are fixed GUIDs**, shared between `appsettings.Development.json`
  and `AcmeDevelopmentPrincipals`. A unit test pins that they match.
- **A tenant provisioned before this step existed gets the step on its next `migrate`.** Its rows
  are inserted only where missing, keyed by code, email or serial.
- **Development-only seam.** `IDevelopmentSubjects` refuses outside Development and outside the
  system scope. A misuse would bind a subject without an invitation.

### What reviewers should examine hardest

- `DevelopmentSubjects` and its effect: that only inserted rows are bound, and that the guards
  hold.
- The step's idempotence and its order: centers are deactivated last, after the invoices use
  the sales centers, and no deactivated customer is invoiced.
- `RelatedDocumentsContributor`: its read-permission filter, and how it handles several targets.
- That the Production test really proves no sample data is written outside Development.
