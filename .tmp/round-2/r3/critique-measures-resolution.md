# Resolution: critique of design-measures.md

1. **Whole-type family — applied.** Whole partials are typed from the plan (`count` `bigint`, an integer `sum` `decimal(38,0)`, a whole `min`/`max` its own family); the reader supplies only a decimal's (p, s) (`MeasureContext.DecimalTypes`, `QueryColumn.Precision` now decimal-only); §5.1 table and intro, §7.2, §8.5, vectors O1/O3/O4/O5/O8/O12 in §7.5.
2. **`HierarchyId` partials — applied.** `min`/`max` over `HierarchyId` rank exactly as text (§2.2 step 5, §4.1, §5.3, §7.1); comparisons over hierarchy paths are refused (§3.1); corpus entry, rank-exactness test and fixture nodes added (§7.3, §7.5).
3. **`and`/`or` — applied; coordinates adjusted.** `and`/`or` stop at the first deciding operand (§3.1, §3.3, §5.4). `Report.MeasureFailed` gains `row` and `column` when C# evaluated the cell (§5.5, 0033 and 0034 plans); an error SQL raised in a summary row carries no cell, because SQL does not name the group, so the parity test compares `pointer` and `reason` only.
4. **Total drill under `top` — adjusted.** Rather than conjoin up to 1,000 keys (an `or` of `and`s for a multi-dimension summary, with no URL budget in 0030 to rely on), a summary's Total row has no drill under `top`; without `top`, flag 5's `having` caveat stands (§6, 0033 §9.4 plan).
5. **Fan-out and memory — applied.** Each leaf row is one leaf cell, folded bottom-up along the row tree, then the column tree (§2.2 step 7); typed columnar `PartialTable` storage (§5.3, §7.2); `MaxPivotCellPartials` 5,000,000 → 413 `Report.TooManyCells` (0033 §8.8, §14 plan); the cancellation token is checked in folding and evaluation (0033 §8.6 plan); a load test at the caps (0033 §15 plan). The budget counts only cells × partials, since leaf rows never outnumber cells.
6. **Scale collapse — applied, studio indicator rejected.** §5.1 states the rule with its vectors and the `sum(Qty) * 1.0000 + sum(Amount)` workaround, and §7.5 pins the vectors. A studio scale flag is not added: a computed decimal's type is known only at run, from the reader.
7. **`SqlDecimal(decimal)` typing — applied, `GetSqlDecimal` rejected.** Every input is rescaled to the partial's (p, s) on arrival (§5.3, §7.2), with the `1.5000m`/`0m` vector (§7.5). Reading partials with `GetSqlDecimal` is not added: the reader and its buffers are spec 0012's, shared by every query, and its 28-digit limit stays with flag F5.
8. **Refusals — applied.** Identity and `null`-literal `cast`s are supported. Over `Date`, the four `diff` functions and the Gregorian `year`/`quarter`/`month`/`day` are supported, with types as the emitter's (§1, §3.1, §3.3, §3.4, §5.1). Flag F1 now lists only what stays refused.
9. **Partial columns reaching the client — applied.** They are requested only when a Total can follow (`totals` and `skip` 0, or an export) and dropped from `ReportTable.Rows` (§6, 0033 §9.2 plan).
10. **Export paging — applied.** An export reads in pages, adds each page's partials, and writes the Total last; ranks are per execution (§4.1, §6, 0033 §12.1–§12.2 plan). Exports totalling at all is new flag F6, since decision 4's "without paging" could be read to cover exports too.
11. **`ARITHABORT OFF` — adjusted.** §5.5 cites 0008 §13.6 and names the symptom. No `SET` or assert is added to 0012's batch: it is already a host obligation for every query, and SqlClient's defaults meet it.
12. **Typeless `PartialValue` — applied.** `PartialValue` and `MeasureProgram` are replaced by `CompiledMeasures`, `MeasureEvaluator`, `PartialTable` and the typed `MeasureValue` (§7.2).
13. **Upgrade and the `totals: true` refusal — rejected.** No stored definition can predate the rule: spec 0033 is unimplemented and ships with it. The soft "not totalled" variant would need a subset check per measure before compiling, plus a second footer, for a handful of refused constructs.
14. **Test gaps — applied.** §7.5 adds precision-39 additions, scale collapse, literal scales, the exact scope of the sign defect, whole families and `int`'s minimum, reader-shaped inputs, short-circuit, preparation per run, and the rank statement with a null group and hierarchy paths. The 0033 §15 plan adds the totals-rule boundary, export-against-screen totals, error parity and the load test. Parity excludes cell coordinates for SQL-raised errors (see 3).
15. **Wording — applied.** The GUID point is in the headline note. `floor`/`ceiling` are marked verified, with the `floor(x) + 0.5` example. The `AVG` row names its `decimal(19,0)` emission. Count and sum adds are checked. QX4011 replaces QX4007 in `Measures`. Ranks are per execution (§4.1).
