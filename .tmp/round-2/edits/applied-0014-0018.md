## Notes for other editors

- **0015 §5.3.** Extras are statements (`AddExtra` takes SQL or a `QuerySpec`). 0018 §4.3's `problems` extra, as ruled, is computed in memory by `RoleService` from the loaded `Permissions` rows, because drift needs `QueryexEngine.Validate` and the registry. 0015 needs one line admitting a service-computed extra (or the ruling revisited).
- **0033 §13.** A seed's `PublishTo` names role codes and grants are written on insert only, but a distribution's role (Acme's `Sales`) can only be created by a distribution step (`Order` ≥ 200), which runs after `core.report-seeds` (`Order` 30). On first provisioning the grant would never be written. 0033 should write grants when the role appears, or order the step after distribution steps.
- **0028.** Its note's Acme additions (`[Assignable]` on `acme.SalesInvoice`, the `assign`/`post` actions, the demo assignments) have no 0018 item; 0018 §1.4 does not list them.
- **0022.** 0022 ships `core.OutgoingEmails` in "a later migration", while 0018 §7's initial migration now holds the 0026/0028/0033/0035 tables (G27); 0022's sentence may want the same treatment.
